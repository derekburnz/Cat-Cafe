# Cat Cafe: note for friends testing the alpha

Thanks for testing! Cat Cafe is an early build, so expect rough edges and placeholder art. Play for 15 to 30
minutes, and tell Derek anything that breaks, confuses you or feels boring.

## What to try

1. Join and follow the hints until you have your own cafe.
2. Catch a stray cat in the Alleyway behind the cafes: walk up, hold E (or the button on a phone), then hit
   Space, click or tap as each circle closes.
3. Carry the cat home and check it in at your cafe door. Put cats to work (Equip Best), and earn tips.
4. Buy an upgrade, then rank up your cafe when the bar fills.
5. Stay through a night: the cafe closes, the dealer's cart shows up, and you can put food in the bowl on your
   back step.
6. Open the Gifts tile, spin the wheel in the street Plaza, and claim a reward in the Index.
7. Leave, come back, and check that your coins and cats are still there.

## How to report a bug

If Settings has a **Report a bug** button, use it: it sends what you type, plus where you were in the game,
straight to us.

## Three quick questions at the end

1. Did you understand what to do in the first minute? What confused you?
2. What was the most fun part, and what was boring or annoying?
3. Fun score from 1 to 5, and would you play again tomorrow?
