## 2. Featured item: Speed Shoes (new)

Derek first asked whether the Lucky Charm was the next featured item. It was not (by the Mystery Market's weekly clock the Long Net was next), and at 12:21 UTC he left the Lucky Charm alone and asked for a new item: "speed shoes that give increased speed while carrying cat for 500 base price and put on sale for friday release you decide how much to boost speed". Model draft first, then test (#3538). Code on #3535.

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Id, name | `speed_shoes`, "Speed Shoes", on Juniper's Mystery Market shelf |
| Card text | "Carry cats faster!" |
| Effect | +3 walk speed while carrying a cat: `CarryWalkSpeed` 16 becomes 19 |
| Full price | 500 golden fish (Derek) |
| Sale | 375 (`MysterySaleShare`, 25% off) |
| Window | `1794607200` to `1795212000` (Friday 2026-11-20 22:00 UTC), then 500 |
| Tier | from Tier 1, bought once |
| Backpack | back to 400 at the start of the window, stays on the shelf |
| Long Net | 320 during the window, featured the week after |

- **Why +3.** A player with no cat walks at 20 and a carrier at 16. At 19 most of the slowdown is gone but a carrier is still a little slower than a free runner, so a tackle chase stays winnable and the 15 minute tackle shield stays the real protection.
- **Stacking.** It adds with the Swift Potion (+4) and Swift Paws. Both of those are added to walking and to carrying alike (walk 20 + boosts, carry 16 + boosts), so with the shoes a carrier is always exactly 1 under the same player's walk and the limit never has to trim anything. Example: with a Swift Potion, walk 24, carry 20, with shoes 23. The catch rush (24 for the first seconds after a catch) is unchanged. One cat or two in the Backpack, same bonus. Nothing with no cat.
- **Limit on carrying (Derek, 2026-11-12: "dont put a flat cap let it stac" at 12:27, then "limit on carrying speeds" at 12:31 UTC).** No flat cap of 20 and everything stacks, but with the shoes a carrier's speed stops at that same player's no-cat walk speed minus 1, counting all their boosts. The limit only trims the shoes' own +3: `carry = max(base carry, min(base carry + 3, own no-cat walk speed - 1))`. The shoes never make a player slower.
- **Switch.** `speedShoes`, off until the Friday restart is confirmed. With it off the item is hidden and cannot be bought.
- **Look.** The owner wears the shoes (`CatCafe_Item_SpeedShoes`). Our own design, no brand look.
- One item is on sale at a time. The server sets the price and the speed. A buy after the window is refused with "The sale just ended" and nothing is charged.
- No timer, no "last chance", no hurry words. Public text says "carry cats faster", not the number.
- Golden fish only, never Robux, nothing random.
- **Lucky Charm:** unchanged, no sale, `luckyCharm` stays off until Derek says.
