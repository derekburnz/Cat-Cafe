## Gavin's model concept (top of the Cat Cafe tab, same day)

How should the alleys work?

- During the night ingame (whenever that is), Cats will randomly spawn in. During the next Night rotation, new cats will spawn in and the old ones will despawn (SIMILAR TO STEAL AND EGG)
- Only certain cats will spawn into specific areas. Each cat will have a rarity/percentage rate of the possibility they will spawn in.
- There will be more locations than just the Alleys (maybe)

How do you catch cats?

- Multiple ways to catch cats? Net, Bait, Rescue

How should the income/cafe

- During the day you earn money by serving food
- You are the Manager and you over see the Cafe and your cat workers

What should be the main gameplay loop and should there be rebirths? (no answer yet)

## Alleys and spawns

**Q1. Night length.** We plan 3 minutes of day and 2 minutes of night on a 5 minute loop, so every session includes a night. Good, or do you want longer days or nights?

> 5 minute days and 5 minute nights

**Q2. Leftover cats.** Cats nobody caught vanish when the next night starts. Can you still catch leftovers during the day? (Our plan: yes.)

> Yes, After the next Night cycle everything refreshes

**Q3. Cats per night.** We plan 6 in the alley and 6 in the park, shared by everyone on the server, first to catch wins. Enough, or should it scale with the number of players?

> Lets do 10 cats per area for now

**Q4. Other locations.** Our order is Park first, then Woods, Rooftops and Harbor. Any places you would rather have, like a beach, school, market, farm or sewers?

> Let's start with 5 locations for now. A Park, woods, Neighbourhood, alleyway, and sewer

**Q5. Rare cats in the alley.** Can a Legendary appear in the alley at tiny odds (our plan: yes, 0.5%), or should rare cats only live in later areas?

> Yes, legendary cats can appear in each area. Lets do 10 different type of cats in each location with different rarities scaling from common, rare, epic, legendary, and mythical

## Catching

**Q6. Net.** The net is the main catch tool with a timed swing, bought and upgraded in the shop (3 levels). A missed swing lets rare and rarer cats run away, Commons stay. OK?

> Lets make it multiple closing circles that pop up with faster timers the rarer the cat and have it be a % chance based on how you do make having a weaker or less upgraded net make the timing harder or more un predictable borderline impossible if its really bad net and a mythical cat if they manage to perfect all circles then it should be 100% if they only hit good then it should be lower etc. have it be a window for good/perfect and then a miss.

**Q7. Bait.** Food in a bowl pulls a cat to you after a short wait, better food gives better odds, and the odds are shown before you buy. Any time of day or only at night? (Our plan: any time.)

> Anytime of day. Lets do after 10 minutes a cat will spawn eating the food and you can catch/tame it or let it go if you don't want it but the food still is used.

**Q8. Rescue.** What did you picture? Our guess: a cat stuck in a tree, a box or a drainpipe that needs a longer hold with no chance of failing, is often rarer, and earns a little more afterwards because it is grateful.

> Cats are trapped in different scenarios depending on the environment they are in. For example cats in the park can be stuck in a tree or a trashcan and cats in alley ways can be stuck in trashbins or stuck in garbage bags. Play some sort of rescue mechanics whether that is timing hitting space on bar or shaking the branch and catching the cat from the tree etc. Make it harder to do the rarer the cat is but also have a higher chance it's a shiny or some sort of variant or differentiatior from the normal cats you catch with a net.

**Q9. Stealing.** Derek said no stealing for now. Are you fine with that for the first version, including no stealing from someone carrying a cat home?

> No, I think players can have a chance to interrupt other players from taking a cat home but only on the way back. Lets do players can only take 1 cat at a time on the way home.

## Income and cafe

**Q10. Money at night.** Does money stop at night when the cafe is closed, or keep flowing? (Our plan: keep flowing. Customers leave and the sign flips to Closed.)

> Yes lets do keep flowing and lets think of an idea about how cats make more money during the day compared to night and add an offline income system. Possibly high chance of customer variants like rich customers come during day with big tips but during night maybe you get a mysterious food dealer that rolls a shop and offers to sell you rare good food to help lure cats only around during night. - changes stock every night and re-rolls the odds can have a upgrade or someway maybe rebirth that makes his odds better of finding rare food

**Q11. The manager's job.** During the day, does the manager do anything hands-on beyond placing cats and buying upgrades, like taking orders, cooking, restocking or greeting a VIP? Or is watching the cafe run enough?

> For now no manager on screen or in the cafe the player is the manager and the cats do the rest

**Q12. Cat jobs.** Do placed cats just sit in spots (counter, window, cushion) and earn tips, or do they have roles like waiter, barista and greeter with different pay?

> Cats can be assigned roles to help run the Cafe. In future planning I want certain cats that have boosts depending their role

**Q13. Other stuff.** Patio and second floor, cat toys, staff outfits, a daily special customer. Which of these matter to you, and what else did you mean by other stuff?

> Content Ideas that can help expand the game more

## Cats

**Q14. How many cats.** 12 in the first test, 40 at launch. More or fewer? Any must-have cats (colors, breeds, names)?

> Depending on how many different areas we want to start off with

**Q15. Look.** Your pictures show cube-headed collectible cats and an upright cat in a suit. Should strays walk on all fours and working cats stand up in uniforms (apron, bow tie, suit), or should every cat have the same body?

> Leaning towards the design for all cats to be able to stand up or change to whatever role they are assigned but strays should look like strays until they are assigned a role

**Q16. Names and special versions.** Should players be able to name their cats? Do you want golden or rainbow versions and a way to merge duplicates? (Our plan: both, after the first test.)

> Players will be able to name their cats. No merging duplicates instead we add shiny versions of each cat
