# Intro cutscene and Tutorial button (F72)

Approved by Derek on 2026-10-27 at 20:45 ("1 yes, 2 yes, 3 keep them but keep it brief"), from his ask at 20:11: a quick camera fly-through of the map before a new player can play, the most important information first because players skip at once, quick scene changes, a small Skip button bottom right after a couple of seconds, and a button at the top that replays it and can be dismissed. Changed by Derek on 2026-10-28 at 00:57: Skip waits 5 seconds, with a bar on it that fills to show when it can be pressed ("slow down the skip button being allowed to be clicked ... show a bar loading it's ability to be pressed make it 5 seconds"), and the intro shows you catching the cat, holding it and running it home ("add a scene of where you catch the cat, of you holding it and running it home"), then quickly giving it a job ("also show you maybe quickly equiping it into a role in the cafe"). Storyboard with a pacing preview: `intro-cutscene-storyboard.html` in the project library folder Tutorial-Agent/ (open it in a browser). Design: Tutorial-Agent thread. Build: Gameplay-Engineer (camera, staging, server flag, landing spot) and UI-Engineer (captions, Skip and its bar, the job card, Tutorial tile, Settings rows).

## 1. Rules

- **Who sees it.** Every player whose profile `introSeen` is false, once, on join. The field defaults to false on every save, existing ones included, so the friends already testing see it on their next join (Derek, answer 2). No migration flips it.
- **When it starts.** After the first ProfileSync that carries a plot. Until then the normal loading cover stays up.
- **Where new players land** (Derek, answer 1). When a player whose `introSeen` is false spawns, the server places the character just inside its own cafe's back door, facing the door (`Layout.cafe.introSpot`, plot-local x -8, z 11; the back door is at x -8 in the back wall at z 14). The character is frozen while the intro runs, so the move is never seen. Replays move nobody.
- **While it runs.** The HUD, world markers and proximity prompts are hidden on this client, the character's controls are off and the camera is Scriptable. The player stands in the safe zone, so nobody can tackle them.
- **Skip** (Derek, 2026-10-28). On the first play the Skip pill is on screen from the start, dimmed, with a bar inside it that fills over 5.0 s. When the bar is full the pill brightens and can be pressed, and a press ends the intro on the spot. A press before then does nothing. On a replay the bar is full at once.
- **The end** (after shot 10 or on Skip). Camera back to Custom, controls, prompts, markers and HUD back on, the first step hint shows as today. The client fires `IntroDone(shot, replay)`.
- **Replay.** The Tutorial tile and the Settings row play it again with Skip ready at once. Nothing is saved except telemetry.
- **Step hints.** `tutorialStep` 0 to 5 and their hints are unchanged. Shots 2 to 4 preview steps 1 and 2 (catch a cat, give it a job).

### 2a. You, the cat and its job (shots 1 to 4)

- **You.** A local copy of the player's own character: set `Archivable` on the character, `Clone()` it, set `Archivable` back; remove its scripts, anchor its root and move it by CFrame, hide its name. It plays the avatar's own run animation while it runs and the carry pose while it holds the cat. It stands at the catch spot from the start: on the line from the stray point to the middle of the yard gate (`Layout.cafe.yardGate`), 3.5 studs from the stray, facing it. The real character stands at the intro spot the whole time and is hidden on this screen from the start until shot 4 ends, through LocalTransparencyModifier set again every frame, since the default camera scripts also write that property on the player's own character. At the cut to shot 5 the copy goes and the real character shows, standing where the copy stopped, so shot 10 lands on it.
- **The cat.** The demo stray is a Tuxedo (an Alleyway Common whose own role is waiter, so it does not read as a second starter Tabby), standing at the stray point through shots 1 and 2 with real strays within 4 studs hidden. At PERFECT plus 0.2 s it hops into your arms over 0.4 s and is held the way a real carry holds a cat: at the carried offset (CatService's `CARRIED_POSE`, moved to a shared module so both sides use one number) with the crossed-arms carry pose Animation-Engineer is building (cat awake, loafing or sitting). Until that pose lands, the intro uses whatever the real carry shows.
- **The run** (shot 3). A 0.25 s turn toward the yard gate, then a run at `CatchRushWalkSpeed` (24, the rush after a real catch) from the catch spot through the middle of the yard gate to the back door (`Layout.cafe.backDoor`), out of sight inside at about 1.4 s. The last steps to the intro spot play at the start of shot 4.
- **The job** (shot 4). At 0.8 s the held cat goes in a small puff and appears seated at the plot's `waiter_1` spot (`Layout.cafe.spots`, the free Tier 1 spot that step 2's hint fills): the seated body (`Catalog.sitModelName`) dressed in the waiter outfit, BowTie and Vest, the way `CatPose.dress` does it (a shared copy of that function is the easy way), with the rate tag live working cats show, reading what a Common waiter earns at Tier 1. If a real cat already works `waiter_1` (a tester's replay), it is hidden for shot 4. You drop the carry pose as the cat leaves.
- Everything here is local and goes at the end, on Skip or on a respawn like the rest. Nothing replicates and no gameplay remote fires.

## 3. Gameplay-Engineer

- Profile `introSeen` (boolean, default false) in the next save version bump, sent in ProfileSync. Settings key `tutorialButton` (boolean, default true) as a ninth `SetSetting` key. (Built.)
- Remote `IntroDone(shot, replay)`: `shot` an integer 0 to 10 (0 when it played to the end, otherwise the shot showing when Skip was pressed), `replay` a boolean; 1 per second. The first non-replay call sets `introSeen` and syncs. Every call logs the custom event `intro_cutscene` with `finished` or `skipped`, the shot and `replay`, so the growth thread can see where people drop off. The shot numbers moved on 2026-10-28, so the event also carries the intro's version (2 from this cut) and old and new drop-offs never mix.
- The landing spot in section 1, applied on CharacterAdded while `introSeen` is false. (Built.)
- The client `IntroController` and `shared/Intro`: ten shots as in section 2, `Intro.SKIP_AFTER = 5`, the staging in section 2a, and the UI calls below. It disables controls and `ProximityPromptService.Enabled`, runs the shots on a Scriptable camera, and restores everything on the end, on Skip or on a respawn mid-intro.
- Streaming is on: `Player:RequestStreamAroundAsync` for the next shot's look point one shot ahead, in a pcall.
- Everything staged is local and removed at the end: your copy and the hidden real character, the demo Tuxedo and the strays hidden near it, the seated waiter and any real waiter hidden for shot 4, the wheel spin, the shot 9 Lighting tween (restore the replicated value), the faded back alley strays and the sunrise clones.

## 4. UI-Engineer

- `Screens/Intro.luau`: letterbox bars (9% of the screen height each, black at about 92%), one caption line bottom centre above the lower bar in FredokaOne with the dark outline (pops from 0.9 to full size in 0.2 s on each shot, at most about 45 characters), ten shot dots on the top bar with the current one gold (the count from `Intro.SHOTS`, not a fixed number), the shot 6 title cards, the shot 2 demo ring drawn with the catch circle look at the stray's screen position, the shot 4 job card, and the Skip pill.
- **Skip with its bar** (Derek, 2026-10-28). The small outlined "Skip »" pill on the lower bar at the right, at least 44 px tall on touch, is on screen from the start of a first play, its label and outline dimmed to about half. A gold bar fills the pill from left to right over `skipAfter` (5.0 s on the first play), steadily. When it is full the pill brightens to full, pops to 1.1 and back in 0.2 s, and from then a tap, a click or gamepad ButtonB skips. A press before then only gives the pill a small shake. On a replay (`skipAfter` 0) it starts full.
- **The job card** (shot 4): a small card beside the cat's screen position with the Collection's four role buttons in miniature (barista, waiter, greeter, entertainer). The given role lights up with a tap 0.25 s after it shows, and the card goes by 0.9 s, as the cat takes its spot.
- Ending: the bars slide out over about 0.45 s while the HUD fades back in.
- Tutorial tile: in the HUD's top right tile row, left of Gifts, the same 58 px square as the others, gold with a "?" and "Tutorial", a small red × in its corner. Tap replays. × hides it, sends `tutorialButton = false` and toasts "Tutorial hidden. Replay it from Settings." Hidden while `settings.tutorialButton` is false, greyed while carrying or holding a catch or rescue, and it glows twice the first time it appears after an intro. (Built.)
- Settings, Screen section: a "Tutorial button" switch bound to `tutorialButton` and a "Replay tutorial" row that plays the intro. (Built.)
- Contract changes on top of the built one in ui-contract.md: `UI.intro.start({ skipAfter, replay })` now shows Skip at once with its bar filling over `skipAfter`; `UI.intro.setShot(index, ...)` and `events.introSkip(shot)` count 1 to 10; new `UI.intro.jobCard(worldPosition, role)` at 0.35 s into shot 4 with `"waiter"`. Final names go in ui-contract.md.

## 5. QA checks

1. A fresh account sees the intro once. Skip's bar fills for 5 s while a press does nothing, then the pill brightens and a press ends the intro at once. The character stands inside its back door with the first hint and its trail showing.
2. A rejoin does not play it again. An existing save plays it once on its next join.
3. The Tutorial tile replays it with Skip ready at once and moves nobody. × hides it, it stays hidden after a rejoin, and the Settings switch brings it back.
4. Shots 2 to 4 show your own avatar catching the Tuxedo, running it home in your arms and the Tuxedo taking the waiter spot in its bow tie and vest. Your avatar never shows twice, and a real waiter hidden for shot 4 is back afterwards.
5. After the intro ends or is skipped at any shot, no copy of you, demo cat, seated waiter, extra stray, spun wheel or night lighting is left, prompts work and the camera follows the character.
6. Skipping in shot 10 sends `IntroDone(10, ...)` and the server accepts it.
7. On a phone-sized screen the captions fit on one line and Skip is easy to tap once its bar is full.
