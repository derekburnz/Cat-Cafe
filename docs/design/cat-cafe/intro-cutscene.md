## 1. Rules

- **Who sees it.** Every player whose profile `introSeen` is false, once, on join. The field defaults to false on every save, existing ones included, so the friends already testing see it on their next join (Derek, answer 2). No migration flips it.
- **When it starts.** After the first ProfileSync that carries a plot, once the Alleyway's camp has streamed in or 5 s have passed (section 3). Until then the normal loading cover stays up.
- **Where new players stand.** At the hometown spawn (0, -14) facing the cannon, like every join (hometown-cannon.md section 5). The intro starts and ends there, so nothing moves the character. The back door landing spot of the first cut goes, since the back door no longer opens onto a catching area. The character is frozen while the intro runs. Replays move nobody.
- **While it runs.** The HUD, world markers and proximity prompts are hidden on this client, the character's controls are off and the camera is Scriptable. The whole hometown is a safe zone, so nobody can tackle them.
- **Roofs** (Derek, 2026-10-28: "roof is see through while inside no need for it", after the roofless cafe in the intro looked wrong). Every roof stays on through the whole intro, as it does in play. Shot 1 cranes over Cafe Street with the roofs on and shot 5 is filmed from inside your cafe under its roof. The intro never calls `WorldController.holdRoofOpen`.
- **Skip** (Derek, 2026-10-28). On the first play the Skip pill is on screen from the start, dimmed, with a bar inside it that fills over 5.0 s. When the bar is full the pill brightens and can be pressed, and a press ends the intro on the spot. A press before then does nothing. On a replay the bar is full at once.
- **The end** (after shot 10 or on Skip). Camera back to Custom, controls, prompts, markers and HUD back on, and the first-flight hint takes over: "Hop in the cannon to fly to the Alleyway!" with an arrow at the cannon (hometown-cannon.md section 3a). The client fires `IntroDone(shot, replay)`.
- **Replay.** The Tutorial tile and the Settings row play it again with Skip ready at once. Nothing is saved except telemetry. A replay ends on the character wherever it stands, and the first-flight hint shows only while its own rule says (until the player's first launch).
- **Step hints.** `tutorialStep` 0 to 5 and their hints follow F84; the intro only hands over to the first-flight hint. Shots 1 to 5 preview the first flight and steps 1 and 2 (catch a cat, give it a job).

### 2a. You, the cat and its job (shots 1 to 5)

- **You.** A local copy of the player's own character: set `Archivable` on the character, `Clone()` it, set `Archivable` back; remove its scripts, anchor its root and move it by CFrame, hide its name. It stands on the cannon's south steps at the ladder from the start, facing the barrel, climbs in at 1.4 s into shot 1 with Animation-Engineer's hop in (until that lands, a 0.4 s hop up to the cannon's `Seat`) and waits hidden at the seat until the BOOM. In shot 2 it flies the arc in the curled ball pose, glides in the clouds and lands on the pad in the crouch landing (`Effects/CannonGlide`), then stands. Between shots 2 and 3 it moves to the catch spot. It plays the avatar's own run animation while it runs and the carry pose while it holds the cat. The real character stands at the spawn the whole time and is hidden on this screen from the start until shot 4 ends, through LocalTransparencyModifier set again every frame, since the default camera scripts also write that property on the player's own character. At the cut to shot 5 the copy goes and the real character shows at the spawn, so shots 8 to 10 see it there and shot 10 lands on it.
- **The cat.** The demo stray is a Tuxedo (an Alleyway Common whose own role is waiter, so it does not read as a second starter Tabby), standing at `S` through shot 3, with real strays within 6 studs of `S` or the run hidden for shots 3 and 4. ## 3. Gameplay-Engineer

- Profile `introSeen` (boolean, default false) in the next save version bump, sent in ProfileSync. Settings key `tutorialButton` (boolean, default true) as a ninth `SetSetting` key. (Built.)
- Remote `IntroDone(shot, replay)`: `shot` an integer 0 to 10 (0 when it played to the end, otherwise the shot showing when Skip was pressed), `replay` a boolean; 1 per second. The first non-replay call sets `introSeen` and syncs. Every call logs the custom event `intro_cutscene` with `finished` or `skipped`, the shot and `replay`, so the growth thread can see where people drop off. The shot field reads "version:shot". The shots change again with F84, so this cut is version 3 (for example "3:4"), and old and new drop-offs never mix.
- **The landing spot goes.** The server no longer moves a player whose `introSeen` is false, and `Layout.cafe.introSpot` can go with F84's back yards.
- The client `IntroController` and `shared/Intro`: ten shots as in section 2, `Intro.SKIP_AFTER = 5`, `Intro.VERSION = 3`, the staging in section 2a, and the UI calls below. Build it with F84 or after it: the shots read `Layout.camps`, `Layout.landingPads`, the cannon's `_Turret`, `Seat`, `Muzzle` and `Fuse` and its moon cover, and the `CampBasket` tag, and F84 removes the gate and back alley numbers the current shots read (`ParkGateZ`, `NeighbourhoodGateZ`, `WoodsGateX`, `SewerGateX`, `AlleyInnerX`). It disables controls and `ProximityPromptService.Enabled`, runs the shots on a Scriptable camera, and restores everything on the end, on Skip or on a respawn mid-intro.
- **Streaming.** With streaming on, a client only holds the parts of the world near its character, and the camps are 2,000 studs from the spawn where the character stays. `Player:RequestStreamAroundAsync` asks the server to send a region early. Before the intro starts (the loading cover still up), request the Alleyway pad `P` and wait for it for up to 5 s; during shots 1 to 4 request the four other camps and the Alleyway board, each in a pcall. Before each camp cut, check that camp's model is there and use the fallbacks in section 2 if not.
- **Effects.** The flight and check-in effects (`Effects/Cannon`, `Effects/Clouds`, VFX-Agent) and the hop, ball, glide and landing poses (`Effects/CannonGlide`, Animation-Engineer) listen to the real character's attributes, so the copy needs a staged call: VFX-Agent adds one that plays a flight on a given model from the hometown cannon to the Alleyway pad on the beats in shot 2 (fuse, BOOM, sky puff, cloud cover, glide, part, landing poof) and the check-in hop into a given basket. Until it lands, the copy flies the arc with no effects and the cut to the camp is a quick fade to white.
- Everything staged is local and removed at the end: your copy and the hidden real character, the demo Tuxedo and the strays hidden near it, the seated waiter and any real waiter hidden for shot 5, the cannon's turn, fuse and moon cover, the wheel spin and the shot 9 Lighting tween (restore the replicated value).

## 4. UI-Engineer

- `Screens/Intro.luau`: letterbox bars (9% of the screen height each, black at about 92%), one caption line bottom centre above the lower bar in FredokaOne with the dark outline (pops from 0.9 to full size in 0.2 s on each shot, at most about 45 characters), ten shot dots on the top bar with the current one gold (the count from `Intro.SHOTS`, not a fixed number), the shot 6 title cards, the shot 3 demo ring drawn with the catch circle look at the stray's screen position, the shot 5 job card, and the Skip pill.
- **Skip with its bar** (Derek, 2026-10-28). The small outlined "Skip »" pill on the lower bar at the right, at least 44 px tall on touch, is on screen from the start of a first play, its label and outline dimmed to about half. - **The job card** (shot 5): a small card beside the waiter spot's screen position, where the cat sits from 0.2 s (the controller passes `waiter_1`'s world point), with the Collection's four role buttons in miniature (barista, waiter, greeter, entertainer). ## 5. QA checks

1. A fresh account sees the intro once. Skip's bar fills for 5 s while a press does nothing, then the pill brightens and a press ends the intro at once. The character stands at the spawn facing the cannon, with the first-flight hint and its arrow at the cannon.
2. A rejoin does not play it again. An existing save plays it once on its next join.
3. The Tutorial tile replays it with Skip ready at once and moves nobody. × hides it, it stays hidden after a rejoin, and the Settings switch brings it back.
4. Shots 1 to 5 show your own avatar climbing into the cannon, flying, landing on the Alleyway camp's pad, catching the Tuxedo, carrying it into the camp, where it hops into the basket, and the Tuxedo taking the waiter spot in its bow tie and vest. Your avatar never shows twice, the cannon is back at rest (and covered at night) from shot 3 on, and a real waiter hidden for shot 5 is back afterwards. Shot 5 is seen from inside with the roof on, no roof fades at any point of the intro, and with a full cafe nothing covers the waiter spot.
5. After the intro ends or is skipped at any shot, no copy of you, demo cat, seated waiter, turned or uncovered cannon, spun wheel or night lighting is left, prompts work and the camera follows the character at the spawn.
6. Skipping in shot 10 sends `IntroDone(10, ...)`, the server accepts it and the event reads "3:10".
7. On a phone-sized screen the captions fit on one line and Skip is easy to tap once its bar is full.
8. On a slow connection (Studio's network emulation, or a phone on mobile data) the loading cover waits at most 5 s for the Alleyway camp; if it is still not there the intro plays without shots 3, 4 and 7, and no shot ever shows empty ground or the sky where a camp should be.
