## What was checked

**Rich tips and night income (#123).**
- **`ClaimRichTip`.**
  - It has a 1 per second limiter.
  - It checks that the caller owns the plot, the record and its serial, that the tip has not expired, and range from the server's `RichTipAnchor` plus 2.
  - `take()` zeroes the record and both attributes before `pay()`, so every path pays once and the marker toasts once.
- **Paying an open tip.** Expiry runs every second. `beforeRelease` credits an open tip before the save on leave.
- **Night income.**
  - `Clock.nightSecondsBetween` matches `phaseAt` second by second.
  - `Economy.settle` splits the window, with nights at 0.6x.
  - Offline pay keeps the day rate.
  - There are no require cycles.

**Lure clean-up (#124, #126).**
- Old saves drop `equippedLure` quietly through `migrate`, so no version bump is needed.
- Nothing in src or tests names `EquipLure`, `lureSpots` or `RescueHoldSeconds` any more.
- The HUD's v0.2 slot and picker are gone, and F and the bowl chip share one `openBait`.

**Role slot labels (#125).** `SlotLabelState` opens a slot exactly as `Economy.spotIsOpen` does (tier only), and it picks the locked slot the server labels. Pills show only in your own cafe, one per role.

**The chase after a miss (F40, #130, #131, #137).**
- **Server.**
  - `flee()` sets `fleeingUntil` (the sprint at StrayFleeSpeed plus StrayRestSeconds, at least GrabRetrySeconds) and `selfRetryAt[userId] = fleeingUntil + StraySelfRetrySeconds` (S44).
  - GrabBegin refuses "It's running!" for everyone, then "Catch your breath, then try again" for the player who missed. Both carry `retry = true` and use the server clock.
- **The run (N84).**
  - The end point is clamped to the area's roam rects. `clearPath` then stops it 1.5 studs short of the first solid part (`RespectCanCollide` since #139).
  - `jumping` holds the roam loop off until `fleeingUntil`.
  - Since #139 the stray moves before `CatchResult` is fired, so `FleeingUntil` reaches clients first.
- **Payloads.**
  - `CatchSchedule` and `CatchResult` lose `pawsLeft`.
  - Effects still reads `success` as the third argument, and Audio skips moments it does not find.
  - #137 removes the paw row, the `Patience` and `Paws_` reads and the v0.3 `catch.showResult`. The rescue bar keeps its paws (section 10b).
- **Client.**
  - WorldController disables the GrabPrompt until `FleeingUntil`. A running cat therefore never takes the one E slot (`Exclusivity` OnePerButton) from a catchable stray next to it.
  - The card reads "Catch your breath..." for the player who missed, from that player's own `CatchResult`, and a blocked press asks the server nothing.

**Shove and knock-loose (F41, #131, #139).**
- **`Bump(targetUserId)`.**
  - `Guard.int` and `GetPlayerByUserId` check the target, and a self-shove is refused (N29).
  - `BumpEnabled` gates the remote, with a 2 per second flood limiter.
  - `Shove.refusal` then runs in section 10c order:
    - the shover is not carrying and holds no lock (N85);
    - one catching area, which the shover's tier opens;
    - range BumpRangeStuds plus 2;
    - the target holds no lock and is outside its own safe zone (plot plus 15 studs round both doors);
    - the 8 s cooldown, the 20 s same-target wait and the 4 s target immunity (S45).
  - The timers live in server tables, and only a landed shove starts them.
- **The push.** The server sends a unit direction, and the target's own client applies `ShoveKnockbackStuds` over 0.25 s. Nobody can choose the strength.
- **Knock-loose.**
  - The roll needs a carry past BumpGraceSeconds and not immune. "held" gives 3 s of immunity.
  - A dropped cat keeps def, charm, variant and the rescued method (the F29 note).
  - The shover can never catch it (S25), and only the old carrier can during the 8 s owner window.
  - A miss inside the window neither flees nor wanders (N86), and tries are unlimited, so it can never leave for good (S26).
  - A night wave keeps a drop younger than 120 s (S32).
  - A re-catch gets no Perfect charm bonus (S36) and is immune for the whole carry.
- **The Shove button.**
  - ShoveController enables it in an open catching area and forwards the press as `Bump`.
  - The button hides while carrying, in the circles and in the rescue bar (N85), on Q or gamepad Y.
  - Since S46 it skips lock holders, players this client shoved in the last 20 s and, for 3 s, a player the server just refused.
- **Display attributes only:** `ShovedAt`, `ShovedBy`, `CarryingCatId`, `CarryStartedAt`, `BumpImmune`, `Dropped`, `DropOwner` and `DropOwnerUntil`.

**Shiny coats (F42, #138).**
- Every server cat clone goes through `AssetRegistry.cloneCat`, with a base fallback, except the stuck cat, which stays base until rescued (N30).
- The 20 `_Shiny` models have the same instance tree as their base models.
- `ShinyChanceNet` and `ShinyChanceBait` are 0.05. The food cards read the bait chance from Config, so they show 5%.

**Shared dealer stock (F43, #141).**
- The seed is integer Park-Miller. Its largest product is about 1.04e14, under 2^53.
- `DealerSlotWeights` is one row (50 / 33 / 14 / 3).
- Bought marks, N32's forward-only clear and S42's recheck at purchase are unchanged.

**Effects for v0.5 (#133).**
- **The gallop and the pant.**
  - The gallop comes from the measured speed of the server's body part, smoothed over 0.1 s, with the stride counted by distance.
  - A sprint at 26 studs a second is about 0.9 studs a step at 30 Hz, under the 3 stud jump threshold.
  - The pant follows `FleeingUntil`.
- **The night curl** is decided on the client from the Clock phase and the `WorkingCat` tag.
- **The shove effects** follow `ShovedAt` and play only under 0.6 s late.
- **The frost burst** plays only when `Shiny` turns true or a shiny `HeldCat` is picked up.
- **Budget.** Dust is 3 per kick at 0.14 s for each running cat within `animatedCats`, through `Particles.burst`, so the phone budget applies.

## Findings

**S46 (should-fix, ui-designer; #131; fixed by c9daa93) The Shove hand stuck on players the server always refuses.** `nearestTarget()` took the nearest player within 6 studs. Around a stray, that player is usually the one catching it, which section 10c refuses. After a shove landed, the same player stayed nearest and was refused for 20 s while a second rival stood close by. `Components/ShoveTarget.pick` now skips lock holders (from the `LockedBy` of strays and rescue spots), this client's targets of the last 20 s and, for 3 s, a target the server just refused.

**N87 (nit, engineer; #130; fixed in #139) Decoration could cut a sprint short.** `clearPath` stopped at any part that answers queries. `params.RespectCanCollide = true` limits it to solid props.

**N88 (nit, ui-designer; raised on #133, fixed in #137) Rate tags over cats curled up at night.** Since the curl is decided on the client, the server's `Napping` stays false and the tags stayed up, showing the day `Rate` while the sign showed the night share. The tags now read "🌙 +$X/s" with `Rate x NightIncomeMultiplier`.

**N89 (nit, ui-designer; raised on #138) Portraits show a shiny cat in its base coat.** `Components/CatPortrait.cloneModel` always clones `Catalog.catModelName(catId)`. For a shiny variant, try `Catalog.shinyModelName(catId)` first, with the same base fallback.

**N90 (nit, ui-designer; raised on #139) A pre-press card for a dropped cat.** During the 8 s owner window, everyone but the owner sees a normal Catch card and is refused with "Its owner gets the first try". The stray carries `DropOwner` and `DropOwnerUntil`, so the card can say so up front, as the running card does with `FleeingUntil`.
