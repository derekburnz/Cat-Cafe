## New findings

**S32 (should-fix, completes the bump sign-off) The night wave still deletes a dropped cat.** Section 9 says that at night start each area "despawns its uncaught strays" and that "dropped (knocked-loose) strays count as uncaught". 10c says a dropped stray stays "until caught or the next night wave". The HUD shows `PhaseEndsAt`, so a griefer can bump a kid's Legendary one second before night start. The owner window never gets used and the cat is gone, which is the loss S26 was meant to stop. Fix: a Dropped stray skips any wave that starts within DroppedMinStaySeconds (for example 120) of its drop, and is removed only when caught or at the first wave after that. Section 9's "count as uncaught" needs the same exception, and so does the "never runs away for good" line in the round 2 question.

**N32 (nit, for the build) Dealer marks across servers.** `os.time()` on two servers can differ by a second or two, so near a window boundary a hop can go from window W + 1 back to W. If the build clears `profile.dealer.bought` whenever the saved window differs from the current one, that hop clears the W + 1 marks and reopens W's slots. Clear only when the current window is greater than the saved one; when it is smaller, treat every slot as bought until the window catches up.

**N33 (nit, designer) Stale wording in features.md.** F23 still lists `NightIndex`, which section 9 and section 13 removed. F29 still says "dropped stray with protect time" (its acceptance column is right). The security list at the end says "once-per-night buys"; it is once per window now.

**N34 (note on N31) BuyLure without a gate is fine.** The reasoning holds: if every Robux product that grants coins is hidden from restricted players, their coins are all earned and food is not a paid random item for them. Word the 2026-10-25 decision as "any Robux product that grants coins", not just "a coins product". Passes that multiply coin income (2x Tips, and in a smaller way Long Nap) are the grey case; settle them under `monetization-policy` when 2x Tips comes back, before it ships.

## Accepted, for the record

Two accounts working together can still get around two rules. Both need two accounts in one 8-player server.

- A friend of the bumper can net the dropped cat once the 8 s owner window ends. That is the design working as Gavin asked (anyone but the bumper may try after the owner), and the owner window, safe zone, grace and immunity are what protect the carrier.
- Two scripts can take turns holding a trap. When one fails, the other grabs the trap the instant `RetryAt` opens, before a person can press, and the first script is clear by the time the second fails. The cat stays stuck and nobody loses anything, but nobody else gets to rescue it. A fair draw for traps (everyone who pressed during the lock goes into a random pick at `RetryAt`) is the fix if QA sees it happen.
