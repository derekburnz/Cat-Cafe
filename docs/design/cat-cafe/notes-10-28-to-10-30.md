# Automatic tips, crate-only decor, dearer food and clearer pictures (F97)

Status: decided by Derek, specced by the game-designer thread, 2026-11-01. These are his notes from 2026-10-28 to 10-30, sent while usage was out and relayed on 2026-11-01. Notes that later specs already cover are listed in section 8, so nothing is done twice.

## 1. Tips collect themselves

Derek, 2026-10-28 16:45: "No need to have a collect tips at front counter menu should be automatic unless its rich customers / trades that have to be accepted in a certain time limit".

- A tip pile pays its owner the moment it appears, wherever the owner is in the game. The till's Collect button and the 8 stud walk-up go.
- The piles still appear on the tables for show. F90's Waiter still sweeps them to the till, where the stack grows and then fades. As each pile pays, a small coin pops over it and the HUD counter ticks up.
- TipPileSeconds, the amounts and TipPilesMax stay. TipPilesMax now only caps how many piles show on the tables, since paid piles no longer wait.
- **Still collected by hand, with a time limit:** the rich visitor's tip, the Cat Collector's offer and the Mystery Visitor's gift. The "if you miss it you miss it" rule (data-model 12, v0.13.4) now applies only to these three.
- **F92 changes:** a pal can no longer collect your piles, because there is nothing to collect. Section 4 of playing-together.md and PalCollectShare go; the pal bonus and Team Rescue stay.
- **Pace:** the pace model already counted the piles' share of income for everyone, so nothing moves. A player who spends the day in the areas now earns about 20% more than before.

## 2. Decor comes only from crates

Derek, 2026-10-28 16:42: "also make it so decor is crate find only".

- The coin decor shop closes. The Window Plants and Cozy Rug join the Classic Crate's Common rows, the Neon Cat Sign its Rare rows and the Fish Tank its Epic rows, with the same bonuses as now (golden-fish.md section 4).
- Players who bought them keep them, placed or not.
- **The Classic page grows to 16 pieces** while every other Decor Index page has 12, and the Classic Crate's chances shift: each Common 8.57%, each Rare 5.4%, each Epic 1.5% (Gameplay-Engineer's figures, #714). The page's full milestone (`decor10`, the gold frame) needs all 16 there, since the frame means the page is complete; its card says "All 16". Opened from Tier 1, the Classic Crate has the whole game to fill it. Duplicates still give fish back.
- The Decor screen's Shop tab becomes "Find more in crates", which points to the Golden Fish Market.
- **The front lawn (F91) stays bought with coins.** It came after this note (10-30), gives no bonus and is how a player picks the look of their cafe front. Derek confirmed it on 2026-11-04 (Set 6, #1001: "do suggested").
- **Pace:** the four pieces were up to +30% money in the pace model. Crates give decor bonuses too, but later and by luck, so a player without crate luck is a little slower. QA's re-time of F95 includes this.

## 4. One rare currency, one name and one picture

Derek, 2026-10-28 16:39: "treats and perks should be remade for pictures also and Golden fish / golden cat coins should be merged together as one currency and picture / name updated".

Today the HUD counter shows a gold coin with a cat face, while every price shows a blue fish, so it looks like two currencies.

- From now on the currency is **Golden Fish**, with one picture used everywhere: a shiny gold fish with a white glint and a happy eye. That covers the HUD counter, every price, prize chips, toasts and the Market's title badge. The cat-face coin and the blue fish go.
- The name stays because every screen, remote and save already says golden fish (`goldenFish`). Set 6 offered Derek the other way round ("Cat Coins" with the cat-face coin); on 2026-11-04 he kept Golden Fish (#1001, "do suggested").
- **New Treats and perks pictures**, each on a gold tile:

  | item | picture |

  | Star Treat | the Star Treat biscuit above |
  | Golden Espresso | a gold cup with a paw in the foam |
  | Big Bowl | a big red bowl with "-2:00" on a little tag |
  | Extra Decor Spot | a dotted square with a plus and a tiny plant (it is blank today) |
  | Catnip | a green sprig |

- **Prize pictures** (Derek's "timing milestone pictures", the Tip Jar): coins become a stack of three gold coins with the amount, golden fish the new fish, spins a small wheel and crates the crate's own render.

## 5. Buffs in place of stars on the cat menu

Derek, 2026-10-28 16:44: "The cat stars don't quite make sense on the menu now so remove that if not necessary and if cats have any specific buffs show that instead inf menu stars not needed".

- The cat room cards, the cat detail and the hover card drop the charm stars.
- In their place a card shows the cat's money per second, plus up to three chips for what lifts it:
  - a heart and "+50% as Barista" in its favourite role, or "Likes: Barista" when it works elsewhere;
  - "Gold +50%";
  - "Lv 12 +22%".
- The detail adds charm as a plain percentage ("Charm +12%" or "Charm -8%").
- Charm stays in the formula. Food cards drop the charm star odds line, since the stars no longer show anywhere. Coins are never sold, so that line is not needed.

## 6. A cleaner Index

Derek, 2026-10-28 17:44 and 17:45: "No need for a spare part of the index has no use" and "also no need to show found/amount/in cafe for index just show found by the picture being filled its black if not found simple no need for writing". His Set 4 Q7 answer asked for the same clean look: normal and gold tabs, simple scrolling through areas, rarity shown and a darker background.

- The Spare cats page goes. Spare cats a player owns still work and rest as normal and show in the cat room with a small "Classic" tag. They do not appear in the Index.
- Each Index tile is just the cat's picture: in full colour if found, a black silhouette if not, on its rarity border. There is no found, amount or "in cafe" text. Tapping a found cat opens its card.
- Two tabs, Normal and Gold. Each tab is one scrolling page with an area header and its ten tiles per area, on a darker background, with the area's reward bar under the header.

## 7. More Epic and rarer cats out in the areas

Derek, 2026-10-29 14:21: "getting epic+ cats feels a little non existent from outside world and only get from bowl".

- Every sunrise wave now has at least one stray of Epic or rarer in each open area. After the wave rolls, if none of its strays is Epic or rarer, the last one rerolls from the Epic, Legendary and Mythical odds only (83 / 15 / 2). It takes a far or nook point, as rarer strays already do.
- Until now about one wave in four in an area had one.
- The sunrise banner adds a line when one is out: "An Epic cat is in the Park!". The Mythical announcement stays as it is.
- With F93's harder circles for rarer cats, an Epic stray is now a race and a challenge between the players in an area. That is the point.
- Same odds everywhere (v0.13.7) still holds, because every area gets the same rule.
