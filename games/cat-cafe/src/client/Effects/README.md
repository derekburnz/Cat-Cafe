# src/client/Effects -> StarterPlayerScripts.Client.Effects

Owner: vfx-animator (Luau reviewed by luau-reviewer); cat motion (`Pose.luau`, `Cats.luau`, and the cat-motion parts of `Logic.luau` and `Presets.luau`: motion, catPoses and nap) is the Animation-Engineer's since 2026-10-27. Preview cat motion outside Studio with `assets/cat-cafe/anim/preview/`. Rules: `.claude/skills/animation-vfx/SKILL.md`. What each effect is for and its status: `docs/vfx/cat-cafe/effects-list.md`.

Everything here runs on this client only. Effects read replicated world state (tags and attributes) and this player's own CatchSchedule, CatchResult, CrateResult, MysteryGiftResult and ProfileSync events. They never fire a remote, never change game state and never move a part the server moves for gameplay; the small turns they draw on a cat's parts stay on this client.

## Hooks for the luau-engineer

1. **Start it** in `Main.client.luau`, after the controllers. Nothing runs until then:
   ```luau
   require(script.Parent.Effects).start()
   ```
2. **Miss (F34)**: when a Miss ends a catch, set `HissAt = Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` on the stray model before the jump-back, so every nearby player sees the hiss (PR #71 does this, then walks the stray 6 to 10 studs away at 24 studs/s). The hop starts as far in as the attribute is late, so it lines up with the walk, and a miss that is already over when it replicates plays nothing. The catcher's own CatchResult already plays it. A one `PivotTo` jump-back works too; the client draws the hop either way.
3. **Naps (F37)**: set `Napping = true` on a cat model while it naps (cats at `NapSpots`, the For Rent doorstep cat); `Pose = "nap"` also works. Anything else, or none, means awake.
4. **Role**: working cats read `Role` (data-model section 13) and fall back to their station's `SpotType` through `SpotId`, which works today.
5. **Rig**: if you weld a cat to its Body to cut replication (reviewer S35 option 2), use Motor6Ds named after their Part1 (`Body`, `Head`, `Tail`, with an anchored `Root`), not WeldConstraints. With WeldConstraints a local turn drags the whole cat.
6. **Streaming (reviewer S37)**: set `ModelStreamingMode = Atomic` on stray and plot cat models so a cat streams in and out whole. Effects copes without it (a cat with no Body yet is tried again every 2 s, and any part arriving makes it bind again whole), but a half-streamed cat cannot move.
7. **For Rent (reviewer N50)**: at a claim the server removes or hides the For Rent dressing for everyone. Effects only draws a copy of the shutters and sign rolling up and fading, and only within 120 studs, so a far player relies on the server's change.
8. **Chase (F40)**: `FleeingUntil` on the stray (server time the sprint and rest end) makes it pant once it stops. The gallop needs nothing: it comes from how fast the server moves the cat.
9. **Tackle (F74, the shove of F41 before it)**: data-model 10c and 13 v0.11. On the press, set `TackleAt = Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` on the tackler's character; when a lunge window ends with no hit, set `TackleMissAt` the same way; on a hit, set `ShovedAt` and `ShovedBy = <tackler's UserId>` on the tackled player's character, as for a shove. The lunge, the knockback and the slide stay yours, on each player's own client. The streak follows the tackler while they move faster than 28 studs a second (a lunge of 12 in 0.35 s is about 34; a Catch Rush walk is 24), so a lunge built slower than that kicks no dust; tell me if you change its speed. Put `TackleLungeSeconds`, `TackleHitGraceSeconds` and `TackledDownSeconds` in `Config` and the streak and the stars keep time with them; until then the design's 0.35, 0.25 and 0.8.
10. **Settings**: call `Effects.applySettings(settings)` wherever `Audio.applySettings` is called (the first ProfileSync and every `UI.settings.changed`), with the switches as shown: `{ screenShake = ..., lowPerformance = ... }`. `screenShake = false` turns off the camera shake of a missed catch and the bump of a shove (a running one stops at once). `lowPerformance = true` swaps in `Presets.budget.lowPerformance` on any device within a second, and `Effects.tier()` then says "low", so the street's customer cap comes down with it. A missing key means shake on and Low performance off, so Low performance must default to off on the Settings screen. Calling it before `start()` or with unchanged settings is fine.
11. **Stray naps (Derek 2026-10-27)**: a stray may nap in a pause of its wander with `Napping = true`. To wake it on its own, set `StretchAt = Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` as you clear `Napping` and wait about 3.6 s before walking it, so it gets up and stretches first; a catch, miss or shove just clears `Napping`.
12. **Getaway and Startled (F55, F57)**: set `RushUntil` (server time the Catch Rush ends) on the catcher's character and `StartledUntil` (server time the slow ends) on the character of the player who missed, as data-model section 13 has them. Each change to a time ahead of now plays; setting either back to 0 or a past time early ends the speed lines. For a skid of dust under each player the getaway pushes, also set `PushedAt = Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` on their character; without it the ring still shows the push.
13. **Night (F66)**: nothing more than the Clock's `AreasOpen` (data-model section 13). The haze and the sparkle play when it turns, never for the first value a client sees, so a player joining at night sees nothing until sunrise.
14. **Codes Cat (F65)**: the station's model carries the `CodesCat` tag with its ground circle inside it named `CodesCircle` (a part or a Model, flat on the ground). Put `CodesCircleRadius` in `Config` and the glow uses the code box's own radius and edges; until then it measures the circle. Only stations in Workspace count, so the tagged template in `ReplicatedStorage.Assets` gets no glow. Nothing else is needed.
15. **Crates and Mythical styles (F53)**: nothing more than what #316 has: `CrateResult(crateId, decorId, duplicate, refund)` to the opener, the `FishMarket` tag on the stall in Workspace (the pop comes off its counter, `Presets.market`, or the stand-in's `FishCrate`), the plot's `Wallpaper`, `Floor` and `Paint` attributes over the shell parts tagged the same, and `DecorId` on each placed piece. The Blossom Tree's petals fall from the box round its child model `CatCafe_Decor_BlossomTree_Canopy` (any child model or part whose name ends `Canopy` or `Blossom`); otherwise from the top of the model.
16. **Potions and the Mystery Visitor (F64)**: data-model v0.10.1 section 13: the character's `ActivePotions` (running ids, oldest drink first; the glow shows the last), `buffs` in ProfileSync for the drink pop, the `MysteryVisitor` and `MysteryGift` tags on the models the client builds under a Plot from `MysteryVisitorUntil` and `MysteryGiftAt`, the plot's `OwnerUserId`, and `MysteryGiftResult(kind, id, count)` to the owner. Effects hooks the result remote up whenever it appears, so a server without F64 is fine. Tag each model as it goes into Workspace (clone it straight under its Plot): a tagged clone parked in ReplicatedStorage and moved in later is seen outside Workspace and gets nothing.
17. **Cat levels (F75)**: data-model 4b and 13 v0.12: keep `Level` (1 to 50) on every cat model under `Plot/Cats` and update it on each level up, as the design has it. A rise on the owner's client plays the sparkle; setting it when you build the model plays nothing, so a cat moving between resting and working never sparkles. `CatLevelUp` is not needed for the effect.

## Budgets

`Presets.budget.low` (touch with no keyboard) and `.high`. Phone: 8 moving cats within 60 studs at 30 updates a second, 4 steady emitters, 3 gold sparkles, 6 butterflies, bursts of 12 at most, 14 confetti pieces, 80 confetti pieces alive at most on any device. The Low performance setting uses `.lowPerformance` on any device: 4 moving cats within 45 studs at 20 updates a second, 2 steady emitters at half their rate, 1 gold sparkle, 2 butterflies, bursts at 35 % and 8 at most, 8 confetti pieces, 2 sets of z's. Speed lines on a player in a Catch Rush: 3 on a PC, 2 on a phone, 1 in Low performance, and only on players within `ambientRadius` (always on this player). Signature effects: 6 cats on a PC, 3 on a phone, 1 in Low performance, within 40 studs, each one cat's emitter rate at Rate 2 or less (split across a prop's parts), and a Jazz Cat adds at most 3 music notes; glowing eyes are a material and cost nothing. The Codes Cat's motes are off in Low performance; its glow stays. Mythical styles: every one in use shows its still picture; 4 move on a PC, 2 on a phone and none in Low performance, within `ambientRadius`, and each moving one takes one of the steady emitters from Ambient, so steam, smoke and fireflies plus Mythical styles never pass the steady budget. Potion glows: 6 players on a PC, 3 on a phone, none in Low performance (the HUD timer still shows), your own first, each one emitter (Steady Paw's split between the hands); the gift twinkle is one emitter, on your own gift only. Everything is chosen by distance once a second (cats twice a second), not every frame, so a budget change shows within a second. Reduce Motion turns off the camera shake and the flash ring, holds the z's still and cuts confetti to 40 %; the Screen shake setting turns off the camera shake alone.

Textures are Roblox's built-in particle sprites until our own are uploaded; put the uploaded id in `Presets.textures` (`goldSparkle` first). No animation ids are used yet, so there is no `AnimationIds.luau` until the first Animation Editor clip.
