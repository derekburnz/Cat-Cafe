Each question has our current plan in it, so a yes is enough. We build the plan as written until he says otherwise.

Catching controls

1. Hold to start. You now hold E for half a second to start a catch, so walking past a cat never starts one by accident. Then the circles pop up and you press Space as each ring closes. Does the hold feel right, or should it be shorter or a single tap?
2. One miss or enough hits. Right now one miss ends the catch and the cat runs off. Should it instead be "hit enough of them", for example 2 of 3 for an Epic, so one slip doesn't lose the cat? Our plan: keep one miss ends it, because tries are unlimited and the chase is part of the fun.
3. Circles by rarity. A Common has 1 circle, a Rare 2, an Epic 3, a Legendary 4 and a Mythical 5, and each ring closes faster the rarer the cat (1.4 seconds for a Common down to 0.65 for a Mythical). Too many, too few or about right?
4. Drop button. While carrying, the Drop button (or G) puts the cat down. In a catching area it lands at your feet and anyone can pick it up with the half-second hold, the same as a cat knocked loose by a shove (your round 3 answer); anywhere else it runs back to its own area. Is that what you'd expect, or should dropping let the cat go for good?

Safe zone

5. Where it starts (picture). Red SAFE ZONE stripes mark where each catching area ends, and on the cafe side nobody can shove. Should the safe zone start at those stripes (our plan), or only at your own cafe door, so the walk home down the street is risky too?

Cats and outfits

6. Themed cats (picture). Derek wants the cats to be more than colours, so every Rare and rarer cat becomes a character with one prop. Alleyway: Pirate Cat (eye patch), Rocker Cat (mini guitar), DJ Cat (headphones), Bandit Cat (eye mask), Ninja Cat (headband), Wizard Cat (wizard hat). Park: Nerd Cat (round glasses), Explorer Cat (safari hat), Sleepy Cat (nightcap), Fancy Cat (top hat and monocle), Fairy Cat (wings), Astro Cat (space helmet). Commons stay plain cats, so a prop means a rarer cat. Any you'd rename, swap or add? Ideas for the Neighbourhood, Woods and Sewer are welcome too.
7. Role outfits (picture). Working cats wear a two-piece outfit for their job: Barista an apron and cap, Waiter a bow tie and vest, Greeter a sash and straw hat, Entertainer a party hat and cape. A themed cat keeps its own prop and leaves off the outfit piece in the same spot, so a Wizard Cat barista wears the apron and keeps its wizard hat. Good?
8. Gold cats. Gold cats are the same cat in gold with a sparkle, and they keep their prop. Should the Legendary and Mythical ones also keep their special effects (Ninja Cat's glowing eyes, Wizard Cat's stars, Fairy Cat's petals, Astro Cat's glow), turned gold? Our plan: yes.

Rewards and shops

9. Daily rewards (picture). A 7-day calendar that moves forward one step each day you play; missing a day never sends you back to day 1. Our plan: coins on days 1, 3 and 5 and foods on days 2, 4 and 6. Day 7 gives a Caviar, a free spin and a Classic Crate, because Derek wants decor to come from crates rather than be handed out. Good, or should day 7 be something bigger?
10. Playtime gifts (picture). Up to 12 gifts you unlock by playing in one session, from 5 minutes up to about 2 hours, each with a countdown. Our plan: coins and foods that get better the longer you play, with free spins on the last ones. Good, or would you rather have fewer, bigger gifts?
11. Spin wheel (picture). A big wheel on the cafe street that you walk up to, not a button (it's in the game now). Everyone gets one free spin a day, and the last two playtime gifts and some Index rewards add more. Spins are never bought with Robux, and every slice shows its chance. Six slices: coins (30% and 18%), 3 Tuna (22%), 2 Salmon (15%), 4 golden fish (10%) and a Cafe Crate (5%). Should a spin ever give a cat? Our plan: no, cats only come from catching, rescuing and bait.
12. Index rewards (picture). Derek asked for rewards worth chasing, so each area's page in the Index has a normal track (3, 6 and all 10 cats) and its own gold track (1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and all 10 gold cats), plus an All Cats track for every different cat you find. Prizes are coins, food, golden fish (our new rare coin with a cat face), wheel spins, Cafe Crates and small "tips forever" bonuses, with a bigger bonus for finishing an area. Anything you'd want for finishing an area or finding every gold cat that isn't there?
13. Codes (picture). Derek wants a Codes Cat on the cafe street: walk up, type a code in the box and it pays once per player, and capitals don't matter. The first code is "playtest" for testers. Got any code words you'd like at launch?
14. Passes (picture). Robux only buys game passes, one-time with the price shown before you buy: Double Tips, an extra cafe room and decor packs. Nothing random is ever sold for Robux, and food is coins only because it decides which cats come. Any passes you'd add or drop?
15. Cafe cosmetics (picture). Decor now comes from Cafe Crates: one per area plus a Classic Crate, bought with golden fish, each holding 10 pieces (wallpaper, flooring, paint, furniture and decorations) that add a small tip bonus while in use, from +2% for a Common to +25% for a Mythical. The odds show before you open one, and a duplicate gives fish back. Our plan: if we ever sell decor packs for Robux, they are looks only, so paying never makes your cafe earn more. Good?

Any you'd rename, swap or add?
