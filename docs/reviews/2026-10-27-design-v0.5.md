# Review: design v0.5 (chase, Shove, shared dealer stock)

Reviewer: luau-reviewer thread, 2026-10-27 about 08:10 UTC. Read only. This covers the security and fairness side of design v0.5 (PR #122, main 7613080), before the engineer builds F40 and F41. It reads data-model sections 8b, 10, 10c, 13, 15 and 17, and features F40 and F41. Follows `2026-10-26-design-v0.3.md` and `2026-10-26-design-v0.3.1.md`.

## Verdict

The design is sound to build with two should-fix items. Both are small server rules that belong in F40 and F41 from the start.

- **Chase.** It removes the shared-paws griefing concern, because tries are unlimited and nothing is taken away. The new risk is a loop that lets one player hold a stray (S44).
- **Shove.** It keeps every v0.3.1 protection. It still needs a per-target limit, so a group cannot juggle one player (S45).
- **Shared dealer stock.** It closes S27 for good, since every server computes the same three foods. One note on the hash is below.

## What was checked

- `GrabBegin` refuses while the stray sprints and rests. `FleeingUntil` is display only, and the server's own table decides (section 13).
- The flee uses server time and moves the server's stray model, so the client never moves the cat.
- `CatchSchedule` and `CatchResult` lose `pawsLeft` and nothing else. The latency cap, index order, first-press rule and S24's gap invariant are unchanged.
- The Perfect charm bonus is still 0 for `flags.autoPress` and for a Dropped stray (S36). That matters more now, because tries are unlimited.
- `Bump` (Shove) still runs every check from 10c. The target id is a `Guard.int`, the target is another player in the server, and the shover is not carrying. Both players must be in one catching area the shover has unlocked, within 6 studs. The target must hold no catch or rescue lock and stand outside its safe zone. The 8 s cooldown, the 20 s same-target wait, the 3 s carry grace, `BumpImmune`, the owner window, the shover's bar from the dropped stray, `DroppedMinStaySeconds` and the off switch are all kept. Every one of these lives in server tables.
- Knockback on the target's client is fine. Only the target's own client can ignore it, which protects nobody but itself, the same trade as client-owned positions. The server should send only a direction and `ShoveKnockbackStuds`, never a strength taken from the shover.
- The dealer seed is `hash(dealerWindow)` with per-player bought marks (N32's forward-only clear is kept). `BuyDealerFood` still recomputes the stock at purchase time (S42).

## Findings

**S44 (should-fix, game-designer and engineer) The chase lets one player keep a stray from everyone.**
- A player can start a catch and miss on purpose, which ends the attempt within a second. When the stray stops, they are at the front of the queue again.
- `GrabCooldownSeconds` (2) is no longer than the sprint plus rest (about 2 s for a Common, 2.7 s for a Mythical). A script that fires `GrabBegin` the moment `FleeingUntil` passes therefore wins nearly every race.
- Each round locks the stray for that player. So a griefer, or a friend saving it for someone, can keep a Legendary away from everyone else in the area for as long as they like.
- This is the v0.2 S20 and v0.3 S28 pattern again.

Fix: the player who missed cannot start `GrabBegin` on that stray until `StraySelfRetrySeconds` (about 1 s) after it stops. Everyone else can try the moment it stops. Keep the wait in the stray's server entry (`selfRetryAt[userId]`), as `RescueService` does for traps. Honest solo play barely notices: at walk speed 20 against a 26 stud/s sprint, a chasing player needs about that long to close the gap anyway.

**S45 (should-fix, game-designer and engineer) Nothing stops a group chain-shoving one player.**
- The 8 s cooldown is per shover and the 20 s wait is per pair. Four players can therefore shove the same target every 2 s and keep them pinned against a wall anywhere in an area.
- Shove now works on anyone, carrying or not, and the audience is young. Being pushed around by a group is the harassment case to design out.

Fix: after any shove lands, its target cannot be shoved by anyone for `ShoveTargetImmuneSeconds` (about 4 s). Keep it in the server's shove table. Only the shove itself is limited; the carry protections stay as they are. A short shield on the target would show it, but the rule does not need one.

**N84 (nit, engineer) Keep a sprinting stray where players can reach it.** "Clamped to its own area" should mean the area's `roam` rects, not its bounds. The alley's roam keeps 5 studs off the outer walls, where the building backs, fire escapes and crate stacks stand. A stray that sprints into that strip, into a prop, or past a gate turn can end up where nobody can catch it, which costs that stray for the rest of the night. Clamp the end point to the roam rects, and stop the sprint short at the first prop the path would cross.

**N85 (nit, engineer and ui-designer) No shoving from inside a minigame.**
- Refuse `Bump` from a shover who holds a catch or rescue lock, not only aimed at one. Otherwise a mistimed key press mid-catch could shove.
- The Shove key must not be E, Space or any key the circles or the rescue bar sink. Hide the Shove button while either one is up.

**N86 (nit, game-designer) Dropped strays: sprint or not?** `features.md` line 155 (the review order) still says "no flee on a dropped stray". Data-model 10c v0.5 says a dropped stray "sprints like any stray" after a miss. Pick one. A sprint eats into the old carrier's 8 s owner window, which argues for keeping a dropped stray still while that window runs.

## Notes, no action needed

- The shared dealer stock is predictable from the time window alone, so anyone can work out every future window's foods. That is harmless while dealer food costs coins. If a dealer item ever costs Robux, `monetization-policy` applies.
- The hash must give the same seed on every server. Keep it integer arithmetic on the window number, like the Park-Miller step in `shared/Dealer.luau`. Never use string hashing or an unseeded `Random`.
- The `autoPress` flag matters more with unlimited tries, because a script now catches every stray it reaches, Mythicals included. Supply (ten strays per area per night, shared) is still the limit. Derek's analytics review of flagged accounts stays the backstop.
