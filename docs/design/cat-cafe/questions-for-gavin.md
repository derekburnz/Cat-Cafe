# Questions for Gavin, round 3 (2026-10-27)

Rounds 1 and 2 are answered and saved word for word in [feedback/](feedback/) (round 2: [gavin-2026-10-27.md](feedback/gavin-2026-10-27.md)), and the design is updated from them (v0.5). These are the quick follow-ups, also in the Cat Cafe tab of the Game Ideas Google Doc under "Questions for Gavin (round 3)". Questions 7 to 21 were added later on 2026-10-27 after Derek's changes that day (hold E to catch, the Drop button, the safe zone, gold cats, role outfits, themed cats and the reward screens from his reference screenshots). Question 22 came with the area drafts that afternoon, and question 17 was updated when the spin wheel became a station in the street. Questions marked "picture" have one Derek pastes under them in the doc, because the Docs connector cannot upload images: Q4 [round3-shiny-options.png](feedback/round3-shiny-options.png), Q11 a Studio screenshot of the SAFE ZONE stripes ([round3-q11-safe-zone.png](feedback/round3-q11-safe-zone.png): the red line under the street arch where Alley Square meets the cafe street; the same stripe runs along both back alleys and the Park end), Q12 the asset thread's themed cat sheet, Q13 the role outfit pieces and Q15 to Q21 Derek's reference screenshots from other games (copies in the project files under Design-Agent/round3-pictures). Each question has our current plan in it, so a yes is enough. We build the plan as written until he says otherwise.

Shove

1. Shoving mid-catch. Can you shove someone while they are in the middle of a catch or a rescue? Our plan: no, they are safe until it ends, so nobody gets shoved into a miss.
2. Shoving a carrier. Does a shove knock a carried cat loose? Our plan: yes, half the time. The carrier gets the first 8 seconds to net it back, and the shover can never catch that cat.

Catching

3. The chase. After a miss the cat sprints off, about 12 studs (two player heights) for a Common up to 32 studs (six player heights) for a Mythical, faster than you can walk, then stops for a second and a half so you can try again. Is that "for a bit", or should rarer cats run farther or hide?

Cats

4. Shiny colours (picture in the doc). Every cat gets its own shiny coat. Which style: A Frost (silver blue), B Gold, C Lavender or a completely different colour for each cat? Our plan: A, Frost. **Answered by Derek, 2026-10-27: B, gold ("change shiny to gold and make it gold modeled").**

Dealer

5. Shared stock. Everyone sees the same three foods. Can each player buy each food once per restock (our plan), or is there a limited amount that everyone races for?

Cafe

6. Night. At night the cafe shows Closed, customers go home and your working cats nap at their stations while still earning 60%. Good?

Catching controls

7. Hold to start. You now hold E for half a second to start a catch, so walking past a cat never starts one by accident. Then the circles pop up and you press Space as each ring closes. Does the hold feel right, or should it be shorter or a single tap?
8. One miss or enough hits. Right now one miss ends the catch and the cat runs off. Should it instead be "hit enough of them", for example 2 of 3 for an Epic, so one slip doesn't lose the cat? Our plan: keep one miss ends it, because tries are unlimited and the chase is part of the fun.
9. Circles by rarity. A Common has 1 circle, a Rare 2, an Epic 3, a Legendary 4 and a Mythical 5, and each ring closes faster the rarer the cat (1.4 seconds for a Common down to 0.65 for a Mythical). Too many, too few or about right?
10. Drop button. While carrying, the Drop button (or G) puts the cat down. In a catching area it lands at your feet and you get the first 8 seconds to catch it again; anywhere else it runs back to its own area. Is that what you'd expect, or should dropping let the cat go for good?

Safe zone

11. Where it starts (picture). Red SAFE ZONE stripes mark where each catching area ends, and on the cafe side nobody can shove. Should the safe zone start at those stripes (our plan), or only at your own cafe door, so the walk home down the street is risky too?

Cats and outfits

12. Themed cats (picture). Derek wants the cats to be more than colours, so every Rare and rarer cat becomes a character with one prop. Alleyway: Pirate Cat (eye patch), Rocker Cat (mini guitar), DJ Cat (headphones), Bandit Cat (eye mask), Ninja Cat (headband), Wizard Cat (wizard hat). Park: Nerd Cat (round glasses), Explorer Cat (safari hat), Sleepy Cat (nightcap), Fancy Cat (top hat and monocle), Fairy Cat (wings), Astro Cat (space helmet). Commons stay plain cats, so a prop means a rarer cat. Any you'd rename, swap or add? Ideas for the Neighbourhood, Woods and Sewer are welcome too.
13. Role outfits (picture). Working cats wear a two-piece outfit for their job: Barista an apron and cap, Waiter a bow tie and vest, Greeter a sash and straw hat, Entertainer a party hat and cape. A themed cat keeps its own prop and leaves off the outfit piece in the same spot, so a Wizard Cat barista wears the apron and keeps its wizard hat. Good?
14. Gold cats. Gold cats are the same cat in gold with a sparkle, and they keep their prop. Should the Legendary and Mythical ones also keep their special effects (Ninja Cat's glowing eyes, Wizard Cat's stars, Fairy Cat's petals, Astro Cat's glow), turned gold? Our plan: yes.

Rewards and shops

15. Daily rewards (picture). A 7-day calendar that moves forward one step each day you play; missing a day never sends you back to day 1. Our plan: coins on days 1, 3 and 5 and foods on days 2, 4 and 6. Day 7 gives a Caviar plus a decor piece. Good, or should day 7 be something bigger?
16. Playtime gifts (picture). Up to 12 gifts you unlock by playing in one session, from 5 minutes up to about 2 hours, each with a countdown. Our plan: coins and foods that get better the longer you play, with free spins on the last ones. Good, or would you rather have fewer, bigger gifts?
17. Spin wheel (picture). Derek wants it as a big wheel on the cafe street that you walk up to, not a button. Everyone gets one free spin a day, and the last two playtime gifts add more. Spins are never bought with Robux, and every slice shows its chance. Six slices: coins (30% and 18%), 3 Tuna (22%), 2 Salmon (15%), 2 Moonlit Sardines (10%) and a Caviar (5%). Should a spin ever give a cat? Our plan: no, cats only come from catching, rescuing and bait.
18. Index rewards (picture). Each area's page in the Index gets a progress bar that pays out as you find its cats: coins at 3 of 10, a food at 6 of 10 and a decor piece themed on that area at 10 of 10. Filling an area's gold page could give something special. What would you want for finishing an area, or for finding every gold cat?
19. Codes and mail (picture). Codes typed in Settings give coins, food or Catnip (never cats) once per player. The prize arrives in Mail along with update gifts. Got any code words you'd like at launch?
20. Passes (picture). Robux only buys game passes, one-time with the price shown before you buy: Double Tips, an extra cafe room and decor packs. Nothing random is ever sold for Robux, and food is coins only because it decides which cats come. Any passes you'd add or drop?
21. Decor skins (picture). Furniture gets skins you pick from a list, unlocked by coins or the Index. Our plan: a skin unlocked with coins or the Index can add a small tip bonus (about 5%), and skins in Robux decor packs are looks only, so paying never makes your cafe earn more. Good?

Any you'd rename, swap or add?
