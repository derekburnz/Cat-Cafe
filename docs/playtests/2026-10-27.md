Testers: 0 humans. Automated Studio run over Studio MCP on Derek's laptop (Studio 0.744, Play Solo, API access off so

## Summary
The single-player loop works on v0.4. Catching, misses and flee, check-in at the back door, night waves and the closed
cafe, both rescue kinds, the shop caps, rank up, shiny, rich tips, travel and bait all behave. One blocker turned up
and is fixed: caught cats' uids contain a hyphen that `Guard.id` rejected, so Place, Unplace, Sell and Rename silently
did nothing for every caught cat (PR #169, verified on 7ce682f and af94fe2). Derek's 2026-10-27 changes (hold E,
no cat on a full miss, Drop and G, SAFE ZONE, Staff tab, My Cafe, Unequip All) work on af94fe2. Open: placement
problems (sunken placeholder strays and traps, For Rent sheets through the counter) and a stale "Walk it home" label.

## Bugs

### [blocker] Caught cats could not be placed, sold or renamed  FIXED in PR #169
Owner: engineer. Cat uids are `GenerateGUID(false):sub(1, 12):lower()` (always with a hyphen) and `Guard.id` accepted
only `^[%l%d_]+$`, so PlaceCat, UnplaceCat, SellCat and RenameCat dropped every caught cat. Only `starter_tabby` worked.
Repro on 775b25c: UnplaceCat on a working caught Tuxedo changed nothing and sent no toast. Verified fixed on 7ce682f.

### [major] Placeholder strays and rescue traps sink into the floor in Neighbourhood, Woods and Sewer
Owner: engineer (placement) / asset-artist (when the real models land). Every wave stray there sits 0.85 studs below
the floor (bottom -0.75, floor 0.10) and lies tilted. Traps: `rescue_neighbourhood_1_Trap` 1.05 studs under,
`rescue_neighbourhood_2_Trap` 4.15, `rescue_woods_1_Trap` and `_2` 3.6, `rescue_sewer_1_Trap` and `_2` 4.15; their stuck
cats are 0.85 under too. Evidence `sink-neighbourhood-strays.jpg`, `sink-sewer-traps.jpg`, `sink-woods-trap.jpg`.

### [minor] For Rent dressing clips through the counter in every empty plot
Owner: engineer. `Plot_N/Dressing/ForRent` overlaps `Plot_N/Counter` in Plot_2 to Plot_8 (around Y 1.7).
Evidence `overlap-forrent-counter.jpg`.

### [minor] Alley wall props overlap the Woods and Sewer gates
Owner: engineer. `CatCafe_Alley_WallWindows` meets `WoodsGate` at (-88, 9, -85), `CatCafe_Alley_WallDoor` meets
`SewerGate` at (88, 8, -85). Evidence `overlap-alleywall-woodsgate.jpg`.

### [minor] "Walk it home" stays on the home button after a drop
Owner: engineer (UI). See the table above.

### [minor] Tier 2 rank up is announced to everyone
Owner: designer to confirm. The plan expects announcements from Tier 3 up.

### [minor] The cafe interior is washed out at night
Owner: engineer (the night lights are made in `WorldController`), asset-artist for the look. With the lamps on, the
inside of the cafe reads almost white and the cats lose their colours (5fe7282, `e10-night-nap.jpg`). Check the
`PointLight` brightness and range inside the plot against the street at night.

### [record] Dealer: a food not in that slot is refused as "Fresh stock just arrived"
`BuyDealerFood` answers "Fresh stock just arrived. Take another look." whenever the food is not in that slot of the
current window (`DealerService`), even when the window has not changed. A real client only sends what its stock shows,
so a player sees this only after a restock; only a forged call (the S13 probe) gets it with no restock. No change
needed unless the reviewer wants a separate wording for a mismatch.

### [record] Park tree stuck cat sits inside the trunk
`Stuck_rescue_park_1`: the cat's bottom is at Y 4.1, with the trunk top at 7.8. Check by eye. Evidence `park-tree-cat.jpg`.
