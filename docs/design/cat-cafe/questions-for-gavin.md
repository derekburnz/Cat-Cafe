# Questions for Gavin, round 3 (2026-10-27)

Rounds 1 and 2 are answered and saved word for word in [feedback/](feedback/) (round 2: [gavin-2026-10-27.md](feedback/gavin-2026-10-27.md)), and the design is updated from them (v0.5). These are the quick follow-ups, also in the Cat Cafe tab of the Game Ideas Google Doc under "Questions for Gavin (round 3)", where question 4 has a picture of the shiny options ([round3-shiny-options.png](feedback/round3-shiny-options.png), recoloured from the asset thread's Tabby render). Each one has our current plan in it, so a yes is enough. We build the plan as written until he says otherwise.

Shove

1. Shoving mid-catch. Can you shove someone while they are in the middle of a catch or a rescue? Our plan: no, they are safe until it ends, so nobody gets shoved into a miss.
2. Shoving a carrier. Does a shove knock a carried cat loose? Our plan: yes, half the time. The carrier gets the first 8 seconds to net it back, and the shover can never catch that cat.

Catching

3. The chase. After a miss the cat sprints off, about 12 studs for a Common up to 32 for a Mythical, faster than you can walk, then stops for a second and a half. Is that "for a bit", or should rarer cats run farther or hide?

Cats

4. Shiny colours (picture in the doc). Every cat gets its own shiny coat. Which style: A Frost (silver blue), B Gold, C Lavender or a completely different colour for each cat? Our plan: A, Frost.

Dealer

5. Shared stock. Everyone sees the same three foods. Can each player buy each food once per restock (our plan), or is there a limited amount that everyone races for?

Cafe

6. Night. At night the cafe shows Closed, customers go home and your working cats nap at their stations while still earning 60%. Good?
