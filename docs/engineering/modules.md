## Remotes (server checks in `CafeService` and `CatService`)

As listed in `src/shared/Remotes.luau` and data-model section 15, plus `RequestSync` (client asks for a full sync) and `GrabResult(id, ok, reason, retry)` (server -> client, closes or keeps the rescue prompt; `id` is a rescueSpotId; strays had it until F93 took their prompt away). `RescueBegin(rescueSpotId)` locks a stuck cat to one player and answers `RescueChallenge(rescueSpotId, challenge)` (kind `bar` or `shake_catch`; a tree sends a second one of kind `circle` after its shakes). `RescuePress(rescueSpotId)` carries no time: the server stamps it on arrival less half the ping and answers `RescueResult(rescueSpotId, hits, pawsLeft, done, success)`. `RescueCancel(rescueSpotId)` gives up. After a failed attempt others wait RescueRetrySeconds (3) and the player who failed RescueSelfRetrySeconds (6).
