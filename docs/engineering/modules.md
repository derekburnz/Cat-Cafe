## Client (`src/client` -> `StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts.Client`)

`Net` (`fire`, `on`), `Controllers/UIController` (`start()`, `state`, `ownDoor()`; forwards `UI.events.renameCat` as `RenameCat` once the UI adds it), `Controllers/WorldController` (`start()`, `zoneAt(position)`; hides a stray's GrabPrompt locally until its `FleeingUntil`), `Controllers/ShoveController` (`start()`: `UI.shove.setEnabled` while in a catching area the tier opens, `UI.events.shove` to `Bump`, and on `ShoveResult` the target's own push of ShoveKnockbackStuds plus `Moments.Shove`, `BumpLoose`, `BumpHeld` with role `shover`, `target` or `bystander`), `Controllers/CustomerController` (`start()`; customers by day only since v0.5 F44, and the ones inside walk out at nightfall). `UI/` belongs to the ui-designer.

## Remotes (server checks in `CafeService` and `CatService`)

As listed in `src/shared/Remotes.luau` and data-model section 15, plus `RequestSync` (client asks for a full sync) and `GrabResult(id, ok, reason, retry)` (server -> client, closes or keeps the catch or rescue prompt; `id` is a strayId or a rescueSpotId). `RescueBegin(rescueSpotId)` locks a stuck cat to one player and answers `RescueChallenge(rescueSpotId, challenge)` (kind `bar` or `shake_catch`; a tree sends a second one of kind `circle` after its shakes). `RescuePress(rescueSpotId)` carries no time: the server stamps it on arrival less half the ping and answers `RescueResult(rescueSpotId, hits, pawsLeft, done, success)`. `RescueCancel(rescueSpotId)` gives up. After a failed attempt others wait RescueRetrySeconds (3) and the player who failed RescueSelfRetrySeconds (6).
Rate limits: 10 calls per second per player per remote, 30 per second across all remotes; `GrabBegin` 1 per 2 s, `RescueBegin` 1 per s, `RescueCancel` 1 per s, `ClaimRichTip` 1 per s, `Bump` 2 per s (ShoveService answers its own cooldowns), `RenameCat` 1 per s (NameService answers RenameCooldownSeconds), `EquipBest`, `RequestSync`, `ClaimOffline` 1 per 2 s, `TutorialAdvance` 1 per s. Over the limit is dropped and logged.
