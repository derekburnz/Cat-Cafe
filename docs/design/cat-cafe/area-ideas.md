## 1. What I looked at

- **Pet Simulator 99's 99 areas.** Each area has its pets plus one thing to do: a machine, a free bonus or a minigame (Green Forest has the Pet Equip Machine, Dark Forest the Potions Upgrade Machine, Mine the Gold Machine, Crystal Caverns a minefield minigame, Cotton Candy Forest free potions, Backyard the Egg Machine). Its woods come in many flavours (Mushroom Field, Enchanted Forest, Firefly Cold Forest, Bamboo Forest), its towns are cosy villages and backyards and its underground areas are mines, caverns and dungeons that glow. Source: [Pro Game Guides, all areas in Pet Simulator 99](https://progameguides.com/roblox/all-areas-in-pet-simulator-99-roblox/).
- **What makes game sewers memorable rather than gross.** Light and life: Hollow Knight's Royal Waterways have glowing streams under a city, Animal Well's tunnels have moss, drifting spores, waterfalls and charming creatures. Blasphemous keeps traces of old grandeur (arches, columns, frescoes). Things to spot: Final Fantasy VII Remake hides retro junk such as fax machines and old post boxes in its piles. Source: [Side Quest, video game sewers](https://sidequest.zone/2025/03/03/video-game-sewers/). Our Sewer already has the glowing canal, waterfalls and a junk town, so the ideas below add old grandeur and things to spot.
- **Cat costumes people buy.** The best sellers are food costumes (sushi, taco, pizza slice, hot dog, banana split), animal hoods (shark fin, lion, duck, dinosaur), wings, capes (royal, vampire) and cowboy outfits. Sources: [Chewy's best cat costumes](https://www.chewy.com/education/cat/general/best-cat-costumes) and [Mental Floss on trending pet costumes](https://www.mentalfloss.com/animals/pets/top-trending-pet-halloween-costumes). Food and animal hoods read well at Roblox scale, so they suit swap ideas.

PS99 gives each area one thing besides its pets, and ours are all the same today (A1: "No new mechanics"). These are ideas to schedule after the three areas are built, each using systems we already have:
