# Gavin's answers, round 2 (2026-10-27)

Gavin answered the round 2 questions in the Cat Cafe tab of the Game Ideas Google Doc. Derek relayed it on 2026-10-27: "gavin answered follow up questions save them and put anymore you might have upload pictures where needed if its asking about models or visuals". The questions below are the ones in the doc; each answer is copied word for word from his bullet. Round 1 is in [gavin-2026-10-26.md](gavin-2026-10-26.md).

## Questions and answers

**Q1. Main loop.** (Round 1 Q17, still open.) By day your cats earn full tips while you assign roles, buy upgrades and set bait. By night you hunt, rescue and carry cats home while the cafe earns 60%. Rank up whenever you meet the next tier. Anything missing?

> Good for now

**Q2. Missing a circle** (changed 2026-10-26 after Derek's playtest). Any Miss ends the catch and the cat jumps back. Each cat gives you a few tries (three for a Common, one for a Legendary or Mythical), then it won't trust you that night. Hitting every circle Good or Perfect always catches it, and Perfects make the cat more charming. Too harsh, too easy or about right?

> Any miss ends the catch and the cat sprints away for a bit making the player catch up to it again to retry. unlimited tries for all cats regardless of rarity but the rarer the cat the harder it is to catch

**Q3. Bumps.** When you are close to someone carrying a cat you can press Bump, and half the time their cat drops and runs a few steps. They get the first 8 seconds to net it back, then anyone except you can try, and a dropped cat never runs away from a failed catch and stays at least 2 minutes, even if night starts. No bumps in the first 3 seconds after a catch or near their own cafe, and a cat that was knocked loose cannot be bumped again on its next trip. Is that the kind of interrupt you meant?

> Instead of Bump, make it a shove mechanic. Players are able to shove other players in areas where they can catch cats

**Q4. Bait bowl.** Your bowl sits at your own cafe and the 10 minute timer keeps going while you are offline. Good, or should bait go out in the areas?

> The bowl sits out side of your cafe and the timer keeps going even if you go offline

**Q5. Shinies.** A shiny earns 1.5x tips and sparkles. 10% chance from rescues, 2% from the net and bait. OK?

> A shiny also has a different color variation. 5% chance from the net and bait

**Q6. Ranking up.** Does ranking up spend the money (our plan) or only need you to have it? Are five tiers enough to start?

> The players must have enough money to spend on the ranking up

**Q7. Roles.** Barista, Waiter, Greeter and Entertainer, all paying the same for now, and each cat has one favorite role that pays 1.5x. Any other roles you want?

> Good for now

**Q8. Night pay.** Cats earn 60% at night. Right amount?

> Cats earn 60% at night because they aren't working and 100% when Cafe is running

**Q9. Dealer.** Each player sees their own three items, restocked every 10 minutes and the same on every server, each buyable once per restock, and higher tiers get better rolls. Or should the whole server share one stock?

> Every server shares one stock

**Q10. Area cats.** Each area has its own 10 cats found nowhere else, opened in the order Alleyway, Park, Neighbourhood, Woods, Sewer. Good, or should some cats show up in more than one area?

> Cats should never show up in different areas they are not meant to be in. There can be multiple of the same cats spawned in the same area but never in different maps.

**Q11. Name.** Is "Cat Cafe" the final name? At least seven Roblox games already use it, which makes ours harder to find in search. One fix is a unique name with Cat Cafe behind it, for example "Purr Pot: Cat Cafe". Any name ideas?

> Cat Cafe will be the place holder for now
