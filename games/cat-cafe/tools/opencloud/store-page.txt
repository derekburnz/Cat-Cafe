Catch a Cat Cafe

🐱 Catch a Cat Cafe is a cozy cat-catching tycoon! Catch strays, bring them home and run your own cat cafe.
🆕 NEW: The Corner Shop is open, with fresh stock every 5 minutes!
🎁 CODES: Release · playtest · group (group members only)

Every stray in town needs a home, and your cafe needs staff.

🥅 Swing your net in 5 areas, from the Alleyway to the Sewer
📘 Collect 50 cats from Common to Mythical, plus rare gold and rainbow coats
☕ Your cats greet customers, make drinks and serve while you grow and decorate your cafe
💥 Ride the Cat Cannon, lure strays with bait and use lucky potions
🤝 Trade cats with friends once your cafe reaches Tier 3
💤 Your cafe keeps earning while you're away

📱 Play on PC, mobile and tablet!
🐾 Join our community group to take home the free Bellhop Cat.

❓ Do I need Robux to get cats? No, every cat is caught by playing.
❓ How do I trade? Reach Tier 3, then tap Trade and pick a player.
