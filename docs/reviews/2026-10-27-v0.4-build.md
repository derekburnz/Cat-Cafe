## Per-frame cost of Effects (#77), the S35 question

Nothing in Effects replicates: client writes on server-owned anchored parts stay on the client, so S35's bandwidth cost cannot come back. The upper bound on a phone is 8 cats x about 10 parts x 30 Hz, about 2,400 local part moves a second in one `BulkMoveTo` per rig. Cats are picked by distance twice a second and ambient sources once a second. Steady particles at the low budget come to about 40 live plus about 7 shiny sparkles, under the skill's 150. `Pose.apply` reads back each part's CFrame after writing it, so a server move always rebases the pose and a client write never does.

## Findings

**S37 (should-fix, vfx, #77; fixed in cb8de25) A cat whose Body had not streamed in was skipped for good.** `Cats.bind` set `skip[model]` forever when `Pose.bind` found no Body. That affected strays picked before their parts arrived on join, and rebinds after the Body streamed out. A late Head or Tail also stayed still while the body moved. The fix: a 2 s retry, a rebind when a BasePart arrives, and no permanent skip. Atomic streaming on cat models is still the clean server-side option.

**S38 (should-fix, vfx, #77; fixed in cb8de25) CatchResult changed shape under Effects.** #71 sends `(strayId, grades, success, pawsLeft, charmGain)`, but Effects read `(strayId, scores, chance, success)` and would have thrown on every result. `Logic.catchSucceeded` now reads both shapes.

**S39 (should-fix, engineer and ui-designer, #71 and #80) Every miss toasts twice.** The server's `finishCatch` sends the paws toast, and `UI.catch.showGrades` sends the same line again. Suggested split: the UI owns the paws toast and the server keeps only the forfeit reasons. The engineer also switches UIController to `UI.catch.showGrades` so `charmGain` and `pawsLeft` reach the overlay.

**N49 (nit, vfx, #77; fixed) Per-frame garbage in Pose.apply and the flutter and confetti loops.** Fixed: tables live on the rig and `around` is at module level.

**N50 (nit, vfx, #77; fixed) The server must remove the For Rent model at a claim.** The shutter roll-up hides the dressing only for nearby clients. A sign's Decal or SurfaceGui does not fade with `Transparency`.

**N51 (nit, engineer, #71) Spec gaps.** Add specs for `Circles.looksHonest` on a partial window (N46) and for `charmGain` when every circle is Perfect.

**N52 (nit, engineer, #71) A stray walks back after its jump.** `wander` circles `stray.home`, which `jumpBack` never moves, so the cat strolls back toward the player who missed. Set `home` to the landing spot.

**N53 (nit, designer) Write down the paw exemptions.** The build exempts a beginner on any Common and the player who lured the stray. Data-model 10 step 5 names only the `dropOwner` and the tutorial Common.

**N54 (nit, engineer, #75, for the jumpBack switch) `clampToArea` can pick a rect across a wall.** Near the junction of the back alleys and the Square, the nearest rect can lie past the end of the south wall, and the straight walk then goes through it. Clamp into the rect the stray stands in.

**N55 (nit, sound and whoever tags the world, #69) Tagged models that stream in late never start their loop.** Either tag BaseParts, or retry the non-near tags in the 0.5 s loop.

**N56 (nit, sound, #69) The hiss sound should follow `HissAt`.** Then every nearby player hears it, not only the catcher, and `CatchFail` keeps only `catchMiss`.

**N57 (nit, ui, #70) Greyed-button bonk on scroll.** `uiError` plays on `InputBegan`, so it also plays when a scroll starts on a greyed button. Play it from `Activated` with `Active` kept true.

**N58 (nit, ui, #78) Announce colours from the text.** A DisplayName with a rarity word in it picks the colour. Use the `Announce` rarity argument once it exists, and skip `uiToast` on announce toasts because Audio plays the chime.
