## Verdict

The two points I raised before the PR are settled the way I asked. Paws are per player per stray in a server table, so one player cannot burn a cat for others, and the dropped-cat rule is restated against paws. The auto-press flag now does something proportionate (no Perfect charm bonus, an analytics event) and clears itself. N44 is in 5b as "or rarer". No blocker. One should-fix (S36): the new charm bonus can be farmed with a friend through bumps. Three nits for the build. Fast travel's shape is the engineer's, and the build list for it is below.

## Findings

**S36 (should-fix, designer) The Perfect charm bonus can be farmed through bumps.** Step 5 adds `PerfectCharmBonus x perfectCount` (doubled for all Perfect) to the stray's charm when it is caught. 10c says a bumped cat drops "with its catId, variant, charm and rescued flag kept". So the charm a Dropped stray carries already includes the bonus from its first catch, and the old carrier, who never runs out of paws on it, gets the bonus again on every re-catch. With a friend doing the bumping (BumpSameTargetSeconds 60 apart), any cat climbs to charm 100 in a few loops: a Mythical gains up to 40 a catch, a Common 8. Charm feeds rating and so income, and it is meant to be rolled once ("never changed after the cat is owned", section 4). Fix, one sentence in step 5: catching a Dropped stray gives no charm bonus; the cat keeps the charm it dropped with.

**N45 (nit, engineer) Keep paws when a player leaves.** Paws are keyed by UserId on the stray and live until it despawns. The `PlayerRemoving` cleanup in CatService already drops that player's locks and circle history. It must not drop `stray.paws[userId]`, or leaving and rejoining the same server through a friend resets a Legendary's one paw.

**N46 (nit, engineer) Clear the auto-press flag only on a full window.** The ring buffer is in server memory, but `flags.autoPress` is saved in the profile. After a rejoin the buffer starts empty. If the clear check runs on whatever is in the buffer, one Good on the first circle reads as 0% Perfect and clears the flag. Evaluate the clear only when the buffer holds the full 50 circles, the same as the set check (`Circles.looksScripted` already needs 50).

**N47 (nit, engineer and UI) Travel during a catch or rescue.** `Travel` is refused while carrying, but nothing says what happens mid-catch. Teleporting out is "out of range", so it counts as a Miss. A kid who taps the Alley button during a Mythical's circles loses its only paw. Refuse `Travel` while the player holds a catch or rescue lock, and grey the buttons with the catch overlay.

## For the F19 Travel build

Server side, per the remote checklist: `destination` through `Guard.id`, then accepted only if it is `home` or a `Catalog.areas` id. Area: `Economy.tier(profile) >= area.tier`. `home` goes to the player's own plot only. Not carrying, no open catch or rescue lock (N47), and TravelCooldownSeconds on the server, plus a `slowLimiters` entry in `Net.luau` so spam is dropped before the handler runs. The landing point comes from the server's Layout, never from the client.
