# Derek's answers to Set 4 and Set 5 (Google Doc, answered by 2026-10-29)

Saved by the game-designer thread on 2026-11-01 before clearing the questions from the Google Doc, as Derek asked on 2026-10-29 17:29 ("All questions from the doc answered so read through those save then delete whatever's there and add new questions with whatever might be needed"). Questions and answers are copied as they stood in the doc; each answer follows its question. Where each answer went is listed at the end.

## Set 4

**Q1. Shoved cats. A dropped cat now walks back to where it was caught, and anyone can pick it up on the way. A cat knocked loose by a shove still stays where it fell (round 3). Should a shoved-loose cat walk home too? Our plan: no, it stays put, so the old carrier can shove back and take it.**

Answer: That works, if a player is shoved they should be knocked back a little also

**Q2. Second props. A themed cat can now wear one or two props. Ideas for the later areas: Skater Cat a backwards cap, Painter Cat a paintbrush in its mouth, Chef Cat a curly moustache, Mail Cat a letter in its mouth, Super Cat a hero mask, Ice Cream Cat sprinkle sunglasses, Scout Cat a ranger hat, Lumberjack Cat a log bundle, Mushroom Cat tiny mushrooms on its back, Firefly Cat glowing antennae, Antler Cat a leaf cloak, Miner Cat a pickaxe, Bubble Cat a rubber duck on its head, Steampunk Cat a gear top hat, Diver Cat an air tank, King Cat a royal cape and Dragon Cat little horns (Unicorn Cat keeps just its horn). Which ones do you want? Our plan: all of them, and the Alleyway and Park cats get second props later the same way.**

Answer: All of them sound good

**Q3. Swaps. From the most popular cat costumes: Taco Cat or Pizza Cat in place of Painter Cat (Neighbourhood), Shark Cat (a shark fin hood) or Duck Cat (a rubber duck hood) in place of Diver Cat (Sewer) and Owl Cat in place of Lumberjack Cat (Woods). Any of these, or keep the 18 as they are? Our plan: keep them.**

Answer: I like the ideas save them but lets keep as they are for now.

**Q4. Something to do in each area. In Pet Simulator 99 every area has one extra thing to do. Ideas for after the three areas are built: a yard sale in the Neighbourhood (two decor pieces for golden fish, new each day), the Fish Pond in the Woods (3 golden fish every 30 minutes) and a Lost and Found box in the Sewer (one free food a day). Want them?**

Answer: I like the idea and its def a good thing it might get annoying to cannon to each location to checkout whatever the benefits are so rethink a way of unlocking and using those new features without making the cannon teleporting between locations tedious, if the cannon between is fast then it wouldn’t be terrible and can show the little feature of each area in the menu and clean it up if needed if not plan out some other possible options on how to have unlocks with every or every couple new tiers.

**Q5. Daily Treat. Day 2 gives 2 Salmon, 15 golden fish and a spin, and day 4 a Caviar, a Classic Crate and a spin. Day 7 gives a Golden Classic Crate (Epic or better decor), a Star Treat (the best bait: a Legendary 45% or a Mythical 15% of the time), 50 golden fish and 3 spins. We picked a Star Treat over a cat crate so cats still only come from catching, rescue and bait. Good?**

Answer: I think the amount of food given out in the game is a little too much right now you never run out and always have too much of the lower tier food so think out other rewards even if its just small amounts of the good currency. But overall good idea.

**Q6. Tip Jar. The 60 minute gift is now 10 minutes of your income, 10 golden fish and a spin. The 120 minute gift is a Caviar, a Classic Crate, 25 golden fish, 2 spins and a Tip Potion. Big enough to keep regulars on for 2 hours?**

Answer: Can probably just have it be a couple of things but make it worthwhile don’t need to overflow the reward panel images with so many things.

**Q7. Index bonuses. Each track now has its own forever bonus: the normal track more tips, the gold track gold luck (finding every gold cat takes the gold chance from 5% to 12.5%) and All Cats faster bait (up to 38% shorter waits). Finishing a track also pays extra crates. The trade off is that the whole Index now adds +35% tips instead of +148%. Good, or should the gold and All Cats tracks keep some tips too?**

Answer: Index needs to be sorted into a normal and gold tab and a cleaner ui for navigating it looks so cluttered right now should be able to just swap between normal/gold and scroll through area’s very simply showing rarity and what hasn’t been found with a black filling and a darker background too make it look clean

**Q8. Group code. Codes are letters and numbers only, so the group code would be "group" (no "!"). It turns on when the game moves to your group and gives members 30 minutes of income, 100 golden fish, 5 spins, a Classic Crate, 2 Caviar and a Tip Potion. Good?**

Answer: Make it so codes can work with symbols like ! or people will start guessing codes too easily that should be fine group migrating will be later down the line anyways and will prompt them to join if they try and enter the code and are not in the community.

**Q9. Potions. Potions come from the Mystery Visitor, the Tip Jar, the Daily Treat and codes. Should the Golden Fish Market also sell them for golden fish? Our plan: not yet, so they stay a nice surprise.**

Answer: I would be down to add them, possibly think there should also be a day time 5min rotating item shop also and then night market can just be a stagnant thing that rotates per server every night.

**Q10. Monetization agent. Should it start now, during the friends alpha, or wait until the game is close to going public? Our plan: wait, so it can use the alpha's playtime numbers.**

Answer: Wait and we can spin an agent for it later down the line and looking when closer to release

## Set 5

**Q1. Net swing backup. Clicking (R2 on a gamepad, a Net button on phones) swings the net, and a hit starts the catch. Should hold E stay as a backup way to start a catch for younger players on phones? Our plan: swing only, with a wide arc in front and a glow on a cat that is in reach. - No hold E swing only is good**

**Q2. Net swing miss. A miss near a cat makes it hop away about a quarter of its normal run: about 6 studs for a Common up to 16 for a Mythical, and a new player's cats never hop before their first catch. OK, or the same short hop for every rarity? -Sounds good whatever you think is best**

**Q3. More circles. Common 3, Rare 4, Epic 5, Legendary 6 and Mythical 7 circles, each about 15% faster. With today's timing bands a Mythical on a Basic Net would be caught about 1 try in 40. Our plan: widen the bands a little so it is about 1 try in 4 (about 4 in 5 with a Master Net) and a Common stays about 19 in 20. Or keep today's bands for the hardest version? - Re-work what the net upgrades do maybe make it so the bands of the circles are bigger with each upgrade but the timing or amount of circles doesn’t change, also currently you can get a miss, then an excellent and the old circles all flash good I think maybe add a bar or text at the top while catching showing how well you did like EXCELLENT pops up with an animation and some vfx at the end of the catch and the old circles should disappear after each one has been hit or missed and at the end of a catch**

**Q4. Harder rescues. Our plan: the marker speeds up a little after every hit and each rarity needs one more hit, so an Epic rescue works about 85% of the time, a Legendary about 4 in 10 and a Mythical about 1 in 8 (now 97%, 73% and 37%). Polish ideas: a paw for the marker, the trap shaking on each hit, hit pips, a timer ring and a burst when the cat is free. Which polish matters most to you?**

Answer: If they are perfect it should always catch, just give less time for the going left and right so people don’t just sit there for perfect timing as for the rest do whatever you recommend.

**Q5. Tier pace. Our plan: raise the rank up costs so Tier 2 stays about 15 minutes, Tier 3 takes about 50 minutes, Tier 4 about 2.5 hours and Tier 5 about 7 hours of play. OK?**

Answer: Sounds good.

**Q6. Coming back. Offline earnings are 25% of your income for up to 1 hour away. Our plan: raise the cap to 8 hours, so a player who comes back the next day gets about 2 hours of income. OK?**

Answer: Raise the cap to 8h works, possible paying options to increase later.

**Q7. Older rare cats. Legendaries and Mythicals from older areas now earn like a cat from the area one step behind your newest (at Tier 5 an Alleyway Legendary earns 87.5 a second, a Sewer Legendary 125). Is that enough, or should they keep up fully with your newest area?**

Answer: I think mythical from T1 should be about as good as legendary from T2 or epic from T3 etc…

**Q8. All locations. We read "all locations should move" as the five catching areas, with the cafes, markets and stations staying in town. Right?**

Answer: Thats correct the catching areas move and the cafe area and town square become a reorganized bigger area with something that blends in the background and borders so its not too plain.
