Purr Pot UI Mockups

Every screen the Luau UI builds, drawn in HTML at the same design sizes so the look can be argued about before anyone opens Studio. Cat portraits are the asset thread's Blender renders. The cafe and alley backgrounds are its vignette renders. Numbers come from the designer's data model v0.3. The first section is new for design v0.3; the sections after it are the v0.2 screens brought up to data v3 (tiers, roles, five rarities). Style v2 (this version) copies the conventions of popular Roblox simulators: thick dark outlines round panels, buttons and display text, shaded button faces with white outlined labels, square icon tiles for the menu, and a red X to close windows.

v0.3: closing circles, tiers and rank up

The net no longer holds. One press on the prompt asks the server for a schedule, then circles close on their rings one after another and the player taps each as it meets its ring. The client sends only which circle was tapped; the server stamps the time and judges it. The green band is Good and the gold band Perfect, drawn from the player's net windows at the closer's speed, so what you see is the real timing. Commons get one circle, Mythicals five.

The card computes everything from the catalog and the player's state, so it needs nothing new from the server. A requirement not met shows "have / need" in grey with a part-filled bar, and the button reads "Not yet" until all three are Done. The server checks every requirement again when Rank up arrives.

HUD in the cafe

Money top left, the tier pill top centre (tap it for the rank up card) with the Cafe, Alley and Areas travel buttons under it, cat count top right with the clock and the next goal card under it, three menu buttons on the left, lure slot bottom right. The next goal card (Pet Simulator 99 quest style) lists what the next tier needs as bars and opens the rank up card when tapped. A red dot on a menu tile means that screen has something new: an upgrade or decor item you can now afford, or a cat you caught since you last opened the book. Opening the screen clears it. Keyboard caps show only when a keyboard is present. The tutorial strip and a toast are shown here at the same time to check they do not collide.

Phone layout and the catch prompt

On a phone the design canvas scales to 0.75 and the floor keeps buttons tappable. No keyboard caps. Near a stray the card reads "Catch" and the round CATCH button appears only on touch; one press drives the real ProximityPrompt and the card shows "Get ready..." until the circles open (section above). The v0.2 hold ring is gone. The chip under the cats pill is the day and night clock (3 minute day, 2 minute night). A stuck cat at night shows a Rescue prompt instead; until the rescue minigames land it is a 4 second hold bar with no miss.

Areas (fast travel)

The Areas button in the travel row opens this panel. One card per area with the share of Epic or better strays there; locked areas are greyed with the tier that opens them. Every Go greys out while you carry a cat, because walking it home is the loop.

Gate signs

A sign hangs over every area gate (Pet Simulator 99 and Bee Swarm style). It shows the lock, the area, the tier it needs and your own progress toward that tier, one bar per requirement, and turns gold row by row as you meet them. A gate two tiers away only says which rank up comes first. Once a gate opens the sign shrinks to a green name plate. Signs show from 50 studs and shrink with distance like a real sign.

Collection

Two tabs. The Book shows every cat in the catalog by rarity, with "?" silhouettes for cats not found yet (12 of the 50 are drawn here); tap a cat for its details. The Cat room lists the cats you own with level, stars, rate and a "Loves" tag for the favorite role, and the Assign, Rest and Sell actions. Sell on a Rare or better asks twice, and the Rest button becomes an equal-sized Cancel while it asks.

Assign on Ginger opens the role picker. The player picks a job, never a slot number: Assign puts the cat in the first free open slot of that role. A role this cat loves shows a star and 1.5x on a gold button; roles that have no open slot yet say which tier opens them.

Lure odds, before you buy

The lure picker opens above the lure slot. Every row prints its five-rarity odds line before any button, and the labelled Details control expands the per-cat split, computed from the catalog so it can never drift from the real table. A bait cat can come from any area your tier has opened, so the split pools those areas and changes when you rank up. Equip and Buy are the same size, and nothing in the shop has a timer.

Popups and toasts

Popups sit above the modals and queue if two arrive together. Cat found is the Pet Simulator hatch reveal: spinning rays in the rarity colour, the cat's own 3D model popping in over a rarity disc and swaying slowly (in game; the Blender render stands in here), a red NEW sticker on a first find, its charm stars filling one by one, and "Purrfect catch! Charm +N" when the catch earned the bonus. It closes itself after five seconds; offline earnings waits for Claim. Toasts drop in under the cafe pill and fade after 2.4 s, four at most.

Fonts
Fredoka One for numbers, titles and buttons; Nunito for everything else. Both ship with Roblox, no uploads.
Scaling
Designed at 1280 x 720. scale = clamp(min(w/1280, h/720), 0.6, 1.3), floor 0.75 on touch. The whole HUD is one UIScale, so everything shrinks together.
Touch
Every tappable thing is at least 44 x 44 design px. Hover effects only with a mouse. Keyboard caps hidden on touch.
Layers
HUD 10, catch prompt 15, modals 20, toasts 30, popups 40. One modal at a time; tap the dim backdrop or press the key again to close.
Code
games/cat-cafe/src/client/UI, 20 files, pure Luau, no packages. UI.demo() runs all of this in Studio with a fake catalog. API in docs/design/cat-cafe/ui-contract.md.
Not final
Decor card art and the coin icon are placeholders. The ring size needs a feel pass on a real phone; the band widths are shared Config numbers (RingGoodWindow 0.80 s, RingPerfectWindow 0.30 s). Room is left for fast travel buttons under the menu and a day and night chip in a top corner.

Feedback that helps most: is anything hard to read over the 3D scene, is the ring band too narrow or too wide, and does the Cat room make sense without reading the doc. Mark anything that should move and the Luau changes in one place.
