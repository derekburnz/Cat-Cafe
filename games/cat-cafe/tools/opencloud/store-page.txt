Catch a Cat Cafe

Catch cats and run your own cat cafe! Swing your net in the Alleyway, the Park, the Neighbourhood, the Woods and the Sewer, and bring home 50 cats from Common to Mythical. Keep an eye out for rare gold and rainbow coats.

Your cats go to work: they take the orders, make the food and serve your customers. Grow from a Corner Cafe to a Grand Cafe, decorate it your way and launch across town in the Cat Cannon.

- Catch strays with your net or lure them with food
- Fill your Index with all 50 cats
- Team up with your pals to rescue stuck cats
- Trade cats with friends once your cafe is big enough
- Daily treats, quests, codes and leaderboards
- Your cafe keeps earning while you're away
