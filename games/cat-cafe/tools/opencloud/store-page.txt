Catch a Cat Cafe

🐱 Catch a Cat Cafe: catch cats all over town, bring them home and run your own cozy cat cafe!

🥅 Swing your net in 5 areas, from the Alleyway to the Sewer

☕ Your cats take orders, make the food and serve customers as you grow from a Corner Cafe to a Grand Cafe
🤝 Rescue stuck cats with your pals, and trade cats with friends once your cafe reaches Tier 3
💤 Your cafe keeps earning while you're away
