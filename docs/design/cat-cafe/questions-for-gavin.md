# Questions for Gavin, round 2 (2026-10-26)

Round 1 (twenty questions) is answered; his answers are saved word for word in [feedback/gavin-2026-10-26.md](feedback/gavin-2026-10-26.md) and built into [gdd.md](gdd.md) v0.3. These are the quick follow-ups, also in the Cat Cafe tab of the Game Ideas Google Doc under "Questions for Gavin (round 2)". Each one has our current plan in it, so a yes is enough. We build the plan as written until he says otherwise.

Loop

1. Main loop (round 1 Q17, still open). By day your cats earn full tips while you assign roles, buy upgrades and set bait. By night you hunt, rescue and carry cats home while the cafe earns 60%. Rank up whenever you meet the next tier. Anything missing?

Catching

2. Missing a circle (changed 2026-10-26 after Derek's playtest). Any Miss ends the catch and the cat jumps back. Each cat gives you a few tries (three for a Common, one for a Legendary or Mythical), then it won't trust you that night. Hitting every circle Good or Perfect always catches it, and Perfects make the cat more charming. Too harsh, too easy or about right?
3. Bumps. When you are close to someone carrying a cat you can press Bump, and half the time their cat drops and runs a few steps. They get the first 8 seconds to net it back, then anyone except you can try, and a dropped cat never runs away from a failed catch and stays at least 2 minutes, even if night starts. No bumps in the first 3 seconds after a catch or near their own cafe, and a cat that was knocked loose cannot be bumped again on its next trip. Is that the kind of interrupt you meant?
4. Bait bowl. Your bowl sits at your own cafe and the 10 minute timer keeps going while you are offline. Good, or should bait go out in the areas?
5. Shinies. A shiny earns 1.5x tips and sparkles. 10% chance from rescues, 2% from the net and bait. OK?

Cafe

6. Ranking up. Does ranking up spend the money (our plan) or only need you to have it? Are five tiers enough to start?
7. Roles. Barista, Waiter, Greeter and Entertainer, all paying the same for now, and each cat has one favorite role that pays 1.5x. Any other roles you want?
8. Night pay. Cats earn 60% at night. Right amount?
9. Dealer. Each player sees their own three items, restocked every 10 minutes and the same on every server, each buyable once per restock, and higher tiers get better rolls. Or should the whole server share one stock?

Cats and areas

10. Area cats. Each area has its own 10 cats found nowhere else, opened in the order Alleyway, Park, Neighbourhood, Woods, Sewer. Good, or should some cats show up in more than one area?

Other

11. Name. Is "Cat Cafe" the final name? At least seven Roblox games already use it, which makes ours harder to find in search. One fix is a unique name with Cat Cafe behind it, for example "Purr Pot: Cat Cafe". Any name ideas?
